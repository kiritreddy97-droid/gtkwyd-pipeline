# Privacy Policy

**Telugu Channel Uploader** (and the "Get To Know What You Don't" uploader) are personal
automation tools used by a single owner, Kirit Reddy, to publish the owner's own videos to
the owner's own YouTube channels. They are not offered to the public.

## What the app accesses
When the owner authorises it, the app requests permission to upload videos and manage the
owner's own YouTube channel (the YouTube Data API v3 scopes `youtube.upload` and `youtube`).
It uses this only to upload videos, thumbnails and titles/descriptions to that channel.

## What is stored
The authorisation token is stored only in the owner's private, encrypted GitHub Actions
secrets and on the owner's computer. No other personal data is collected.

## Sharing
The app does not collect data about viewers or any other users, and does not sell, share or
disclose any data to third parties. Videos are published to YouTube by the owner's own
choice and are subject to YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

## Revoking access
The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
