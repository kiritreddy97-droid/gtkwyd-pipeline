## Raw Materials
In most factories, tiny balls are crushed from tungsten carbide powder into perfect spheres.
[[tungsten carbide powder]] [[metal spheres manufacturing]]
{{A 3D render of fine grey metal powder pouring into a circular steel mould}} {{A 3D render of tiny metal spheres rolling down a smooth steel tray}}

## Housing Mold
Precision brass or steel sockets are hollowed out to hold the rolling metal sphere.
[[brass pen tip manufacturing]] [[metal socket pen tip]]
{{A cutaway 3D render of a brass pen tip showing a hollow channel inside}} {{A metal lathe machine carving a shiny brass cone for a ballpoint pen tip}}

## Ball Fitting
The hard sphere is snapped inside the brass cone and locked tight with pressure.
[[ball inserted in pen tip]] [[pen tip assembly]]
{{A 3D render showing a tiny steel ball pressed securely into the tip of a brass pen cone}} {{A cutaway 3D render of a ballpoint tip showing tiny gaps around the metal ball}}

## Thick Ink
Special thick ink is mixed so it stays solid until the rolling ball moves.
[[ballpoint pen ink mixing]] [[viscous ink]]
{{A 3D render of thick dark blue liquid stirring inside a clear glass container}} {{A close up 3D render of a drop of thick blue ink suspended on a metal surface}}

## Filling Tube
Injectors pump the viscous blue ink directly into clear plastic ink reservoir tubes.
[[filling pen ink tube]] [[pen ink cartridge plastic]]
{{A 3D render of a narrow metal nozzle filling a clear plastic tube with dark blue ink}} {{A row of transparent plastic tubes filled with dark blue ink on an industrial rack}}

## Plastic Body
Plastic pellets are melted and molded to form the pen outer barrel and clip.
[[plastic molding pen barrel]] [[pen plastic casing]]
{{A 3D render of colourful plastic granules melting inside an injection mold machine}} {{A clear 3D render of a freshly molded plastic ballpoint pen barrel resting on steel}}

## Final Assembly
Machines press the ink tube, spring, and brass tip together inside the plastic body.
[[assembling ballpoint pen]] [[pen components exploding view]]
{{An exploded 3D view showing the outer barrel, spring, ink tube, and brass tip aligned}} {{A 3D render of automated mechanical arms snapping a complete ballpoint pen together}}

When writing, paper rolls the ball, transferring a thin coat of ink smoothly.
