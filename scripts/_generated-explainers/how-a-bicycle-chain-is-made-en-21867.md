## Tiny Powerhouse
A single bicycle chain is built from over eight hundred individual steel components.
[[bicycle chain macro]] [[bike chain components]]

## Stamping Plates
High speed stamping presses punch outer and inner side plates from rolls of tough steel.
[[metal stamping press]] [[steel chain plates]]
{{a steel strip being punched into figure eight bicycle chain plates inside a factory press}} {{freshly stamped steel bicycle chain outer plates falling into a metal collection bin}}

## Forming Rollers
Thick steel wire is cut and shaped into tiny hollow rollers and solid cylindrical pins.
[[steel wire cutting]] [[chain pins and rollers]]
{{a machine cutting thick steel wire into small cylindrical bike chain pins}} {{a pile of small polished steel rollers for a bicycle chain on a metal tray}}

## Heat Treating
In high temp furnaces the steel parts are heat treated to survive extreme pedal tension.
[[industrial heat treating]] [[steel hardening furnace]]
{{steel bike chain parts glowing orange inside a hot industrial heat treating furnace}} {{hardened dark steel bicycle chain plates cooling on a conveyor belt}}

## Surface Polishing
Vibrating machines polish the parts with ceramic stones to remove rough edges and burrs.
[[vibratory polishing machine]] [[polishing metal parts]]
{{a high angle view of steel chain parts vibrating in a tub with ceramic polishing stones}} {{smooth shiny polished bike chain plates sitting next to rough unpolished ones}}

## Automated Assembly
Robotic assembly lines press the pins rollers and plates together into a continuous chain.
[[automated chain assembly]] [[robot assembling bike chain]]
{{a robotic assembly arm pressing steel pins into bicycle chain plates with high precision}} {{a continuous bike chain moving along a mechanical track as pins are inserted}}

## Hot Lubrication
The finished chains pass through hot oil baths so lubricant reaches deep inside every joint.
[[industrial oil bath]] [[lubricating bike chain]]
{{a bicycle chain dipping into a bath of warm golden lubricating oil}} {{a freshly oiled bicycle chain dripping excess fluid on a wire mesh rack}}

## Power Transfer
On your bike the rollers engage gear teeth to transfer your leg power smoothly.
[[bike chain gear engagement]] [[bicycle cassette and chain]]
{{a cutaway 3D view of a bicycle chain roller fitting perfectly between cassette teeth}} {{a shiny bicycle chain rotating smoothly around a metal rear cassette cog}}

## Extreme Strength
Despite weighing just half a pound a quality bike chain can withstand thousands of pounds.
