## Melting Metal
In most factories, raw copper and zinc are melted together to create strong brass.
[[melting brass furnace]] [[molten brass metal]]
{{3D render of glowing molten brass pouring from a factory crucible}} {{3D render of bright yellow brass alloy ingots stacked neatly}}

## Extruding Bars
The molten metal is cast into long, solid brass rods and cooled down completely.
[[brass rod factory]] [[extruding metal bar]]
{{3D render of a thick brass rod emerging from an industrial machine}} {{3D render of long polished brass bars resting on metal rollers}}

## Cutting Bodies
Heavy industrial saws slice the brass rods into individual rectangular lock body blocks.
[[metal cutting saw]] [[brass block slicing]]
{{3D render of a sharp circular blade cutting through a thick brass bar}} {{3D render of a freshly sliced rectangular brass padlock body on a tray}}

## Drilling Holes
Precision drill bits carve out internal chambers for the key cylinder and steel shackle.
[[drilling metal lock]] [[cnc brass drilling]]
{{3D render of drill bits carving precise holes into a brass lock body}} {{3D cutaway render showing deep drilled channels inside a brass block}}

## Inserting Pins
Small copper pins and tiny steel springs are dropped into the key chamber precisely.
[[padlock pin tumbler]] [[lock internal mechanism]]
{{3D cutaway render showing tiny brass pins and springs inserted into holes}} {{3D close up of tiny lock pins resting inside the brass body cylinder}}

## Bending Shackle
A hardened steel rod is bent into a U-shape to create the curved shackle.
[[metal shackle bending]] [[hardened steel shackle]]
{{3D render of a hydraulic arm bending a steel rod into a U-shape}} {{3D render of a shiny chrome U-shaped padlock shackle resting on metal}}

## Final Assembly
The steel shackle clicks into place, and the lock body is sealed tightly forever.
[[assembling padlock]] [[padlock locking mechanism]]
{{3D render of a steel shackle snapping into a brass lock body}} {{3D cutaway render showing the internal locking latch clamping the shackle}}

The lock opens only when unique key grooves push every pin to perfect height.
