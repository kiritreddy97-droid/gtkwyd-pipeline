## Metal Sheets
Workers start with flat sheets of durable stainless steel or gold alloy.

## Stamping Out
Heavy industrial presses stamp out the flat silhouette shapes from the metal.
[[metal stamping press]] [[stamped nib blanks]]
{{a heavy industrial stamping machine pressing down onto a shiny sheet of metal}} {{flat metal blanks shaped like fountain pen nibs scattered on a metal table}}

## Piercing Hole
A small hole is pierced near the center to control the ink flow.
[[breather hole nib]] [[punching metal hole]]
{{a precision mechanical punch creating a tiny round hole in a flat metal nib blank}} {{a close up of a flat fountain pen nib blank with a small round breather hole}}

## Curved Shaping
Machines curve the flat metal blanks into a rounded three dimensional dome shape.
[[bending metal nib]] [[curved pen nib]]
{{a mechanical press curving a flat metal nib blank into a rounded dome shape}} {{a curved metal fountain pen nib blank showing its new three dimensional form}}

## Tipping Point
A microscopic pellet of hard precious metal is welded onto the very tip.
[[welding nib tip]] [[precious metal pellet]]
{{a tiny welding laser attaching a microscopic metal pellet onto the tip of a nib}} {{close up of a fountain pen nib tip with a welded silver metal ball}}

## Cutting Slit
A diamond saw cuts a razor thin slit straight through the center tip.
[[sawing nib slit]] [[cutting metal slit]]
{{a thin spinning diamond saw blade cutting a precise slit into a fountain pen nib}} {{close up of a fountain pen nib with a razor thin vertical slit down the center}}

## Mirror Polish
Workers polish the finished nib until it shines like a mirror.
