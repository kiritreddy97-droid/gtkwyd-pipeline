## Raw Materials
Pure silica sand, soda ash, and limestone are melted down at extreme temperatures.
[[silica sand]] [[glass melting]]
{{a pile of pure white silica sand next to chunks of soda ash on a metal tray}} {{glowing molten glass glowing bright orange in a heavy industrial furnace container}}

## Float Process
Molten glass flows onto a molten tin bath to create a perfectly flat sheet.
[[molten tin bath]] [[float glass sheet]]
{{glowing hot molten glass spreading out smoothly over a silvery liquid tin bath surface}} {{a smooth continuous ribbon of hot orange glass moving forward in a factory}}

## Deep Clean
The cooled glass sheet undergoes an intense washing process with pure deionized water.
[[glass washing]] [[clean glass sheet]]
{{a large flat glass sheet passing under multiple rotating industrial cleaning water jets}} {{a perfectly clean transparent glass sheet drying under strong air blowers}}

## Silver Coating
A thin layer of actual silver is chemically deposited right onto the glass surface.
[[silver coating]] [[mirror silvering]]
{{a chemical liquid flowing over a glass sheet creating a brilliant metallic silver coating}} {{a shiny reflective silver film forming instantly on the surface of the glass}}

## Copper Layer
A protective copper layer is applied over the silver to prevent oxidation and damage.
[[copper layer]] [[protecting silver]]
{{a chemical solution creating a shiny bronze and copper colored layer over the silver}} {{a uniform copper coating covering the reflective silver backing on a glass sheet}}

## Paint Sealing
Industrial safety paint is sprayed on the back to seal and protect the metallic layers.
[[mirror paint backing]] [[sealing glass mirror]]
{{rollers applying a thick coat of dark green protective paint over the copper backing}} {{a large sheet of glass coated with green protective paint moving down a conveyor}}

## Edge Cutting
Diamond cutting wheels shape the mirror and grind smooth edges for safe handling.
[[cutting glass mirror]] [[smooth glass edges]]
{{a spinning diamond cutting wheel slicing cleanly through a layered glass mirror sheet}} {{a close up of a robotic grinder smoothing the sharp edges of a glass mirror}}

## Pure Reflection
Light passes through the glass, reflects off the silver, and bounces right back.
