## Forming the Core
The black paste is pressed through a narrow nozzle to create long thin strings.

## Baking the Lead
After drying, these flexible rods are baked in intense heat until they become rock solid.
[[industrial oven baking metal]] [[factory kiln heat process]]
{{A 3D render of graphite rods sliding into a glowing red industrial baking furnace.}} {{A close up 3D render of hardened graphite rods cooling down after high heat treatment.}}

## Grooving Wood Slats
Meanwhile, thin slats of soft cedar wood are carved with precise parallel grooves.
[[wooden slat carving woodworking]] [[factory wood routing machine]]

## Inserting the Cores
Glue is applied to the grooves, and the hardened graphite leads are dropped right inside.
[[automated factory assembly line]] [[glue application industrial machine]]
{{A 3D render of glue dispensing neatly into the grooves of a flat cedar slat.}} {{A 3D render showing dark graphite rods placing perfectly inside the glued wooden grooves.}}

## The Sandwich Press
A second grooved wood slat is pressed on top to form a solid wooden sandwich.
