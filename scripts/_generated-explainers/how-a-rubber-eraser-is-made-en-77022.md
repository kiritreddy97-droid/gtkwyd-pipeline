## Raw Materials
Raw rubber is mixed with sulfur and vegetable oils for increased material flexibility.
[[raw rubber]] [[rubber mixing]]
{{raw blocks of natural rubber sitting on a clean industrial factory floor}} {{a mixture of liquid rubber and sulfur being stirred in a large vat}}

## Heating
The mixture is heated under intense pressure to create a durable, bouncy material.
[[rubber heating]] [[industrial press]]
{{a large industrial heated press machine for rubber}} {{a block of rubber inside a metallic heating chamber}}

## Extrusion
A machine forces the soft rubber through shaped molds to form long strips.
[[rubber extrusion]] [[extruder machine]]
{{a machine extruding a long strip of pink rubber}} {{a cross section of a rubber extrusion machine}}

## Cutting
Sharp industrial blades slice the long rubber strips into small individual eraser blocks.
[[rubber cutting]] [[industrial blade]]
{{a sharp metal blade cutting a long strip of rubber}} {{a pile of freshly cut rubber eraser blocks on a conveyor belt}}

## Smoothing
Rotating drums tumble the blocks to remove sharp edges and soften the surfaces.
[[tumbling machine]] [[polishing rubber]]
{{rubber eraser blocks tumbling inside a large rotating metal drum}} {{a close up of smooth edges on a finished rubber eraser}}

## Quality Test
Technicians test each batch to ensure the rubber is perfectly firm yet soft.
[[quality control]] [[testing rubber]]
{{a mechanical press testing the firmness of a rubber eraser}} {{a rubber eraser being bent to test its elasticity and flexibility}}

## Packaging
The finished erasers are sorted and wrapped before heading to your pencil case.

## Friction
The eraser creates friction, lifting graphite dust away from the paper's porous fibers.

## The Payoff
Your mistake disappears because the eraser acts like a tiny magnet for lead.
