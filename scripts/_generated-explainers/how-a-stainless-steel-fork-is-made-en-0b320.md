## Blanking
A heavy industrial press punches out flat metal outlines called fork blanks.

## Rolling
Rolling machines squeeze the metal to make the handle thick and the front thin.

## Cutting Tines
Another powerful metal die cuts out the spaces between the fork's sharp tines.
[[stamping fork tines]] [[metal die cutting]]

## 3D Shaping
A curved mold presses the flat fork to give it its classic bent scoop shape.

## Polishing
