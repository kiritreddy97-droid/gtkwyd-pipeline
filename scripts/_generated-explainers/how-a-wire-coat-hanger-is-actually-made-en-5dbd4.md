## Straightening
First, heavy steel rollers uncoil the thick metal wire and press it perfectly straight.
[[wire straightening machine]] [[industrial metal roller]]
{{steel wire passing through a series of heavy metal rollers in a machine}} {{a straight steel wire exiting a metal straightening mechanism, clean studio lighting}}

## Cutting
Next, a high-speed mechanical blade snips the continuous steel wire into precise equal lengths.
[[cutting steel wire machine]] [[wire cutter factory]]
{{a sharp industrial blade chopping a straight steel wire into exact segments}} {{straight steel wire rods dropping into a clean metal collector bin}}

## Base Bending
Automated mechanical arms grip the straight metal rod and instantly fold the bottom triangular base.
[[wire bending machine]] [[metal hanger forming]]
{{robotic mechanical pegs bending a steel rod into a triangular hanger shape}} {{a steel rod bent into a flat triangle on a clean metal workbench}}

## Twisting Hook
Two rotating pins grab the loose wire ends and tightly twist them together to form the neck.
[[wire twisting mechanism]] [[coat hanger neck twist]]
{{close up 3D render of two steel wire ends being tightly twisted together by spinning pins}} {{a clear view of the freshly twisted neck of a metal coat hanger}}

## Hook Curling
A curved mechanical die catches the top straight tip and curls it into a smooth hook shape.
[[wire hook bending die]] [[forming hanger hook]]
{{a round metallic die curving the top of a steel wire into a classic hanger hook}} {{a fully shaped metal wire coat hanger hanging on a clean steel pin}}

## Coating
In most factories, the bare metal hanger gets dipped in smooth plastic enamel or liquid vinyl.
[[hanger plastic dip coating]] [[dip coating process]]
{{a raw steel coat hanger being lowered into a tank of bright white liquid plastic coating}} {{a newly vinyl coated white wire hanger dripping dry on an automated conveyor line}}

## Heat Curing
The freshly coated wire hangers travel through a hot oven to bake and harden the outer layer.
