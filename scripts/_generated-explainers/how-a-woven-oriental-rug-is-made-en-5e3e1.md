## The Secret
A genuine oriental rug can take over a full year to weave completely by hand.

## Spinning Wool
First, raw sheep wool is cleaned, carded, and spun into strong, tight yarn threads.
[[spinning wool yarn]] [[carding raw wool]]
{{a 3D render of clean fluffy white raw wool being spun into long thread}} {{a 3D render of wooden spindles winding freshly spun wool yarn spool}}

## Dyeing
Next, weavers submerge the wool yarn into large vats of natural colorful dyes.
[[dyeing wool yarn]] [[colorful dye vats wool]]
{{a 3D render of skeins of wool yarn dipping into a steaming blue dye vat}} {{a 3D render of brightly colored dyed wool yarn skeins hanging to dry}}

## Setting Loom
Strong cotton strands are stretched vertically across a massive wooden loom frame to form warp.
[[weaving loom vertical warp]] [[loom threads setup]]
{{a 3D render of a large vertical wooden loom with tight white cotton warp threads stretched across}} {{a close up 3D render of parallel cotton threads tied tightly on a loom frame}}

## Knotting
Artisans meticulously tie thousands of individual wool knots around pairs of vertical warp threads.
[[tying rug knot yarn loom]] [[hand knotting rug weave]]
{{a close up 3D render of gloved hands tying a single red wool knot onto vertical loom strands}} {{a 3D render showing a row of tiny tied yarn knots attached to cotton warp threads}}

## Combing
After each row of knots, horizontal weft threads are inserted and packed tight using heavy combs.
[[packing rug knots comb]] [[weaving loom comb tool]]
{{a 3D render of a heavy metal rug comb pressing yarn knots down firmly on a loom}} {{a 3D render showing horizontal weft thread sliding between vertical warp strands above knots}}

## Shearing
Special curved shears trim the pile flat, revealing the crisp, intricate pattern underneath.
[[trimming rug pile shears]] [[shearing wool rug surface]]
{{a 3D render of sharp curved metal shears cutting uneven wool yarn fibers flush}} {{a close up 3D render of a smooth trimmed carpet surface next to untrimmed yarn loops}}

## Washing
Finally, the rug is washed with water and wooden paddles to soften the dense wool.
[[washing woven rug water]] [[cleaning oriental rug carpet]]
{{a 3D render of clean water pouring over a woven rug being scraped with wooden paddles}} {{a 3D render of a damp patterned oriental rug drying under bright warm studio lights}}

## Endurance
Because every single knot is self-locking, these rugs can easily endure over a century.
