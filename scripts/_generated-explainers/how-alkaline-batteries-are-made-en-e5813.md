## Power Secret
Have you ever wondered how tiny chemicals inside a metal can generate portable electric power?

## Steel Can
First, automated presses punch nickel-plated steel disks into hollow cylindrical battery cans.
[[metal pressing machine]] [[steel battery casing]]
{{A heavy metal press stamping a flat metal sheet into small steel battery cans.}} {{A row of shiny hollow nickel-plated steel battery cans moving on a conveyor belt.}}

## Cathode Mix
In most factories, dark manganese dioxide powder is pressed firmly inside the steel outer casing.
[[manganese dioxide powder]] [[battery cathode layer]]
{{Dark black manganese dioxide powder being compacted into a cylindrical steel battery can.}} {{A cutaway view showing the black manganese powder lining the inner wall of a battery.}}

## Separator
A thin paper separator tube goes inside to keep the positive and negative sides apart.
[[paper separator tube]] [[battery assembly]]
{{A white paper separator tube being inserted into the hollow center of a battery casing.}} {{A 3D close up of the white paper lining inside the dark battery cavity.}}

## Anode Gel
Next, a thick gel containing fine zinc powder and liquid potassium hydroxide is injected inside.
[[zinc gel injection]] [[battery core filling]]
{{A metal nozzle injecting a gray zinc chemical gel into the center of a battery.}} {{A cutaway showing gray zinc gel filling the middle cavity of the battery core.}}

## Sealing
A steel pin attached to a plastic cap is inserted to seal the entire battery.
[[battery nail insert]] [[battery sealing cap]]
{{A brass collector pin being inserted through a plastic top seal into the battery.}} {{A machine crimping the top edge of a metal battery can tightly shut.}}

## Labeling
Machines wrap a decorative plastic sleeve around the cell and shrink-heat it tight.
[[battery label application]] [[heat shrink plastic sleeve]]
{{A colorful plastic sleeve sliding over a shiny silver alkaline battery casing.}} {{Hot air shrinking the plastic label tightly around the cylindrical battery body.}}

## Power Flow
When connected in a circuit, electrons flow from the zinc anode to the manganese cathode.
[[battery electron flow]] [[battery circuit 3d]]
{{A 3D animation style illustration showing glowing blue electrons flowing out of a battery top.}} {{A battery placed in a simple circuit lighting up a small bright light bulb.}}
