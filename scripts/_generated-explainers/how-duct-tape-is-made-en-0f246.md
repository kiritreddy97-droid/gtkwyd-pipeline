## The Backing
It all starts with a thin outer layer made of flexible polyethylene plastic.

## The Mesh
Next, a tough fabric mesh called scrim is bonded directly to the plastic film.

{{a close-up render of a woven cloth mesh fabric layer placing onto plastic}} {{a 3D macro view showing cross-hatched fabric threads bonding to a smooth grey film}}

## The Adhesive
In most factories, heavy-duty natural rubber adhesive is melted in large heated tanks.
[[industrial mixer tank]] [[liquid adhesive vat]]
{{thick liquid yellow rubber adhesive swirling inside a clean steel industrial tank}} {{a cross-section of a steel vat mixing thick sticky rubber coating material}}

## Coating
Massive steel rollers coat the fabric and plastic layers with the sticky hot rubber.
[[roller coater machine]] [[industrial tape manufacturing]]
{{heavy steel factory rollers applying a coat of thick glue onto a moving grey sheet}} {{a side view of grey tape material passing between two heavy polished steel rollers}}

## Cooling
The long layered sheet then passes over chilled rollers to instantly solidify the adhesive.
[[chilled roller machine]] [[factory cooling rollers]]
{{a long grey sheet wrapping around large cold metal rollers emitting subtle steam mist}} {{a side render showing the hot sticky tape passing over frosty blue cooling cylinders}}

## Jumbo Roll
The finished triple-layer tape is wound onto a giant spool called a jumbo roll.

## Slicing
Sharp circular blades slice the giant roll into hundreds of standard individual widths.
