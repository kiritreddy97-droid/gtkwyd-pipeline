## Wavy Cutters
Special slitting blades slice the sheet and create iconic wavy strands of noodles.

## Steam Cooking
High temperature steam cooks the raw wavy noodles completely in under two minutes.
[[steaming noodles factory]] [[noodle steam tunnel]]

## Flash Frying
Dipping the cooked noodles into hot oil rapidly dehydrates them and creates microscopic pores.
[[frying instant noodles]] [[noodle basket fryer]]
{{metal wire baskets holding noodle blocks submerged in bubbling golden frying oil}} {{a 3D cutaway showing hot oil dehydrating a noodle block and creating micro pores inside}}

## Cooling Down
Powerful air fans rapidly cool the crispy fried noodle blocks back to room temperature.
[[cooling conveyor noodles]] [[factory cooling fans]]
{{crispy golden noodle blocks riding a mesh belt under powerful industrial cooling fans}} {{a clean 3D render of golden fried noodle blocks cooling down on a conveyor belt}}
