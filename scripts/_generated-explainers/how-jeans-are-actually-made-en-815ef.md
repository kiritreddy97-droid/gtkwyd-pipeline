## Indigo Dyeing
Next, only the vertical warp threads are dipped into deep indigo dye baths.
[[indigo thread dyeing]] [[blue yarn spools]]
{{a 3D render of white cotton threads dipping into a vat of blue liquid dye}} {{a 3D render of dyed dark blue cotton yarn winding onto large spools}}

## Weaving Denim
Industrial looms weave blue threads over and under white threads to create durable denim.
[[weaving denim fabric]] [[denim weave close up]]
{{a 3D render of a mechanical loom weaving blue and white threads together}} {{an extreme close up 3D render of diagonal blue and white denim weave pattern}}

## Cutting Patterns
High-precision automated lasers or blades cut the denim rolls into individual jean panels.
[[cutting denim fabric]] [[jeans pattern pieces]]
{{a 3D render of a laser beam cutting precise leg patterns from denim cloth}} {{a 3D render of flat cut denim pattern pieces laid out neatly}}

## Stitching the Panels
Heavy-duty sewing machines stitch the denim pieces together using thick orange thread.
[[sewing denim jeans]] [[orange thread seam]]

## Adding Hardware
Metal rivets and sturdy buttons are pressed in to reinforce high-stress pocket corners.

## Washing and Finishing
Washing the stiff new jeans with pumice stones creates that soft worn-in feel.
