## The Secret
Every giant plastic soda bottle starts its life as a tiny, thick test tube.

## Raw Resin
Factories melt down millions of tiny clear plastic beads called PET resin pellets.
[[PET plastic pellets]] [[melting plastic resin]]
{{a close-up 3D render of transparent PET plastic pellets pouring into a steel hopper}} {{a warm glowing industrial extruder heating up clear plastic resin}}

## Injection
The molten plastic is injected into molds to create these hard preform tubes.
[[injection molding plastic]] [[plastic preform mold]]
{{a steel mold opening to reveal newly formed plastic preform tubes}} {{a row of clear plastic preforms moving along an automated factory conveyor belt}}

## Reheating
Before shaping, these preforms travel through intense infrared heaters to become soft again.
[[heating plastic preform]] [[infrared bottle heater]]
{{plastic preforms passing by glowing red infrared heating elements in 3D}} {{a cutaway showing a soft glowing plastic preform inside a heating chamber}}

## Stretch Blowing
High-pressure air shoots inside while a steel rod stretches the plastic downward.
[[blow molding process]] [[stretch blow molding]]
{{a 3D cutaway of a metal rod stretching a plastic preform inside a bottle mold}} {{high pressure air expanding a soft plastic preform inside a metal bottle mold}}

## Cooling
Cold water chilled metal walls instantly cool and harden the newly blown bottle.
[[cooling bottle mold]] [[mold chilling]]
{{a metal bottle mold opening to release a fully formed clear plastic soda bottle}} {{a newly shaped clear plastic bottle cooling inside a metal mold cavity}}

The five-bump bottom gives the thin plastic maximum strength to hold pressure.
