## Corrugation
Heavy hydraulic presses bend the flat steel sheets into strong wavy corrugated panels.

## Frame Assembly
Thick steel beams are welded together to form the container strong outer frame.

## Corner Castings
Extremely solid cast steel corner blocks are attached to allow easy crane lifting.

## Welding Walls
The corrugated side panels are securely welded onto the rigid structural steel skeleton.

## Wooden Floor
Treated marine plywood boards are screwed directly down onto the bottom floor joists.

## Protective Paint
The entire container gets sandblasted and sprayed with thick anti-corrosion marine paint.
