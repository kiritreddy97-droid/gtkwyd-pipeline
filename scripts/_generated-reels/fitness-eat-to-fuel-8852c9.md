## The Role of Digestion
Digestion takes priority over energy usage when you eat before a workout. This can leave less energy available for your exercise.
[[person eating a meal, with a clock ticking in the background]] [[digestion process animation]]

## The Energy Cost
Gastrointestinal processes like digestion and blood flow to the stomach consume energy. This reduces the amount of energy available for your workout.
[[energy cost graph]]

## The Optimal Window
Eating a meal 1-3 hours before a workout allows for energy replenishment. This reduces the energy cost of digestion and allows more energy for exercise.
