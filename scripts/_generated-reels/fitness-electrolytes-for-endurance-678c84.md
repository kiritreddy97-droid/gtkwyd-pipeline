## The role of electrolytes
When you sweat, you lose not only water but also essential electrolytes like sodium, potassium, and calcium. These minerals help regulate muscle and nerve function, and their loss can lead to weakness, cramping, and fatigue.

## The impact on exercise performance
As electrolytes are lost, your body's ability to regulate muscle contractions and relaxations is impaired. This can lead to decreased performance, reduced power output, and increased risk of injury.
