## The issue
Sitting for long periods of time tightens the hip flexors, leading to poor posture and reduced mobility. When the hip flexors are tight, it can affect the entire lower body.
[[person sitting at desk with crossed legs]] [[gym training]]

## The fix
Kneeling on all fours with hands under shoulders, take one knee out to the side and lower it down towards the ground. This gentle stretch can help lengthen the hip flexors and improve range of motion.
