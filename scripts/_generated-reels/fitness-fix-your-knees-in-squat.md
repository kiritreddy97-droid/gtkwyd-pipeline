## The issue
Knees caving in during a squat can put unnecessary stress on the joints and lead to injuries. Even small variations in knee alignment can add up over time.
[[person performing a squat with knees caving in]] [[gym training]]

## The fix
Keep the weight in line with the toes by pushing the knees out gently, in the same direction as the toes. A single cue, "knees out," is often enough to correct it immediately.
[[person performing a squat with knees out]] [[close up of legs during squat]]

## Why it matters
Proper knee alignment reduces the stress on the joints and helps maintain healthy knees for the long term.
