## The problem
Rounded shoulders are a common posture issue, caused by a variety of factors including poor sitting form and muscle imbalances. When shoulders roll forward, it can put strain on the neck and back.
[[person sitting with rounded shoulders]] [[computer or desk setup]]

## The fix
Pulling the shoulder blades down and back, keeping them in line with the spine, helps to correct the posture. A simple cue, "down and back," is often enough to retrain the muscles.
[[person sitting with corrected posture]] [[shoulder blades squeezing down]]

## Why it matters
Better posture reduces pressure on the spine and improves breathing, leading to increased comfort and reduced fatigue.
