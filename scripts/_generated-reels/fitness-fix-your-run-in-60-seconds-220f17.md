## The common issue
Knee pain or a feeling of your calf muscles "locking up" can be a sign of overstriding or tight calf muscles. This can lead to poor running form and put unnecessary stress on the joints.
[[person running with poor form and knee pain]] [[gym training]]

## The fix
Stretching your calf muscles can help improve your running form and reduce knee pain. A simple calf stretch involves standing with your feet shoulder-width apart and leaning forward at the waist.
[[person standing in a calf stretch position]] [[close up of calf stretch]]

## Why it matters
By stretching your calf muscles, you can reduce the risk of injury and improve your overall running efficiency.
