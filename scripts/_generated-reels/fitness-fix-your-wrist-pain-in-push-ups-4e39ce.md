## The problem
Wrist pain during push-ups is common, often caused by poor hand positioning or inadequate forearm strength. This can lead to discomfort and even injury.
[[person doing a push-up with wrist pain]] [[gym training]]

## The fix
Keeping the forearms in a neutral position, with palms facing forward, reduces strain on the wrists. A gentle "forearm down" cue can help maintain proper alignment.
[[person doing a push-up with proper forearm alignment]] [[close up of hands during push-up]]

## Why it matters
Proper hand positioning and forearm strength can prevent wrist pain and promote better overall form.
