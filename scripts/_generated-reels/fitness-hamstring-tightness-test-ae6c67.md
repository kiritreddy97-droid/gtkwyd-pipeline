## The test
To check if your hamstrings are too tight, try this simple test: sit on the floor with your legs straight out in front of you. If you can't bend your knees, it may be a sign that your hamstrings are overactive.
[[person sitting on floor with legs straight]] [[person bending knees in tight hamstrings]]

## The verdict
If you can bend your knees easily, your hamstrings are likely not too tight. If not, it may be worth exploring ways to increase flexibility.
[[person bending knees easily]] [[person struggling to bend knees in tight hamstrings]]

## What's next
If your hamstrings are too tight, try incorporating some hamstring stretches into your routine, such as the kneeling hamstring stretch or the seated hamstring stretch.
