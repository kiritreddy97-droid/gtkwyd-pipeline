## The role of carbs during rest
Carbohydrates are broken down into glucose and used to fuel the body's resting metabolic processes, such as maintaining body temperature and supporting non-essential functions. The body relies on glucose for energy even at rest, as it's the primary source of energy for the brain and other organs.

## The switch to fat during low-intensity exercise
During low-intensity exercise, such as yoga or walking, the body starts to use stored fat for energy instead of glucose. This is because the intensity of the exercise is not high enough to require the rapid energy production that glucose provides.

## The carb boost during high-intensity exercise
However, when the exercise becomes high-intensity, such as sprinting or weightlifting, the body quickly switches back to glucose as its primary source of energy. This is because glucose provides a rapid and efficient source of energy that's essential for high-intensity activities.
