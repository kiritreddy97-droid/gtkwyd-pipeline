## The mistake
Focusing too much on the front knee can cause it to extend too far forward, throwing off the entire lower body alignment. This can lead to a loss of balance and increased risk of injury.
[[person lunging with front knee extending forward]] [[gym training]]

## The fix
Keeping the weight evenly distributed between both feet, with the right foot in line with the left, provides a stable base for the lunge. This simple adjustment can make a huge difference in the depth and control of the movement.
[[person lunging with right foot in line with left]] [[close up of feet during lunge]]

## Why it matters
A stable lunge position reduces the impact on the joints and allows for a more effective workout.
