## The Role of Protein
After a workout, your muscles are torn and need repair. Protein plays a crucial role in this process.
[[person lifting weights, with a protein shake in hand]] [[muscle fibers under tension]]

## How Protein Helps
Protein provides the building blocks for muscle growth and repair. It helps to stimulate muscle protein synthesis, which is essential for muscle recovery.
[[protein-rich foods, such as chicken and fish, on a plate]] [[muscle protein synthesis animation]]

## Why It Matters
Adequate protein intake after a workout can help you recover faster and build muscle more efficiently.
