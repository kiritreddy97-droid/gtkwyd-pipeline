## Why Resting Matters
Resting between sets allows your body to recover and build strength, not just the muscles used during the set. During rest, your body repairs and rebuilds muscle tissue, which is essential for progress.
[[person resting between sets, eyes closed, and relaxed]] [[timer ticking on screen]]

## How Resting Affects Performance
If you don't rest between sets, you're not only delaying recovery but also reducing performance. Your body is fatigued, leading to decreased strength and endurance, which can put you at risk of injury.
