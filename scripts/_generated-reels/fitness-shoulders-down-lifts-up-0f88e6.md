## The mistake
When shoulders are rolled forward, the weight is lifted with less optimal leverage, putting unnecessary strain on the lower back. This can lead to poor lifting form and increased risk of injury.
[[person lifting with rolled shoulders]] [[gym training]]

## The fix
Keeping the shoulders down and away from the ears engages the core and traps more effectively, reducing strain on the lower back. Maintaining a relaxed shoulder position also improves overall lifting efficiency.
[[person lifting with proper shoulder alignment]] [[close up of shoulders during lift]]

## Why it matters
A downward-tilting shoulder position helps to transfer force more efficiently from the legs to the upper body, making lifts feel lighter and more controlled.
