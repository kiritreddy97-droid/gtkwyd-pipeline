## The Neglect
Many of us assume that a good night's sleep is enough to help our bodies recover from a tough workout. But neglecting sleep can actually impede muscle recovery just as much as a bad workout.
[[person sleeping in bed]] [[gloomy bedroom with dark curtains]]

## The Link
During sleep, our bodies repair and rebuild muscle tissue, making it stronger. Without adequate sleep, this process is slowed, and our muscles may not recover as quickly.
[[person sleeping on a mattress]] [[brain with neurons active]]

## The Consequence
Poor sleep quality can lead to decreased muscle strength, increased soreness, and reduced performance.
