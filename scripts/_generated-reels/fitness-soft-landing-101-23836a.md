## The mistake
Hitting the ground with your knees bent or your weight forward is the most common way to land incorrectly. This can put unnecessary stress on your joints, leading to potential injuries.
[[person jumping and landing awkwardly]] [[gym training]]

## The fix
Keeping your knees straight and your weight evenly distributed on both feet, with your arms wrapped around your body, reduces impact and stress. A soft landing is achieved by landing midfoot or forefoot, absorbing the shock with your muscles.
[[person jumping and landing softly]] [[close up of person landing midfoot]]

## Why it matters
A soft landing reduces the risk of injury and helps maintain healthy joints over the long term.
