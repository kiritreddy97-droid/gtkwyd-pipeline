## The challenge
Pacing cardio can be mentally exhausting, especially when you're new to steady state exercise. It's easy to get caught up in the moment and push yourself too hard.
[[person running with a high heart rate]] [[congested cityscape]]

## The solution
Breathe in for two counts, hold for two counts, exhale for two counts, and hold again for two counts - a 2-2-2 pattern. This simple rhythm helps you find a consistent pace and avoid burnout.
[[person running with a steady pace, breathing in sync]] [[calm landscape with a runner in the distance]]

## The benefit
By focusing on your breathing, you can tune into your body's needs and maintain a consistent pace.
