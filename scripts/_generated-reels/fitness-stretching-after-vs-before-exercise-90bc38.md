## Before Exercise
Static stretching before exercise can decrease muscle power and endurance. It can also reduce the ability to perform at high intensity.
[[person holding a static stretch pose]] [[gym training background]]

## After Exercise
Dynamic stretching and foam rolling after exercise, on the other hand, can improve muscle recovery and reduce muscle soreness. It can help restore range of motion and reduce muscle stiffness.
[[person foam rolling after exercise]] [[gym recovery area]]

## Why the difference
After exercise, our muscles are in a state of heightened sensitivity and inflammation. Stretching in this state can help promote blood flow and reduce muscle spasms.
