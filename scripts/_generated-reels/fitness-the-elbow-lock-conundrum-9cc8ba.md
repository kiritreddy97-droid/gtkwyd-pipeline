## The mistake
Locking your elbows during a plank is a common habit that can lead to strain on the joints. It can cause the shoulders to be pulled down and the chest to be compressed, leading to a less-than-optimal plank position.
[[person with locked elbows in a plank position]] [[gym training]]

## The fix
Keeping the elbows slightly bent and in line with the shoulders is a more effective and sustainable way to maintain proper form. This allows the chest to expand and the shoulders to relax, reducing strain on the joints.
[[person with elbows slightly bent in a plank position]] [[close up of arms during plank]]

## Why it matters
Maintaining proper elbow position in a plank can help prevent strain on the joints, reducing the risk of injury and improving overall form.
