## The impact
Mild dehydration can cause decreased blood volume, reduced blood flow, and impaired delivery of oxygen to the muscles. This can lead to reduced energy levels and decreased focus during exercise.
[[person exercising, looking tired]] [[dehydrated person holding an empty water bottle]]

## The fix
Adequate hydration can help regulate body temperature, transport nutrients and oxygen to the muscles, and support the removal of waste products. Even mild dehydration can be reversed with proper hydration and electrolyte balance.
[[person drinking water before exercise]] [[electrolyte-rich drink being poured into a bottle]]

## The result
Proper hydration can improve exercise performance, enhance focus, and reduce fatigue. By paying attention to your body's hydration needs, you can optimize your energy and focus during exercise.
