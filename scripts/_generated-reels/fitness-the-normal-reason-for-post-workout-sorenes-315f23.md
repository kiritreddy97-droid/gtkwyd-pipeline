## What's happening
When you exercise, tiny tears occur in your muscle fibers, and your body needs to repair them. This process involves inflammation and the breakdown of damaged tissue.

## The repair signal
The next day, you may feel soreness, stiffness, or fatigue, which can be mistaken for a weakness or a failure. However, this soreness is actually your body's way of signaling that the repair process has begun.
