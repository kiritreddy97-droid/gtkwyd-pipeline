## The mistake
Narrowing the grip too much can cause the elbows to flare outward and disrupt the movement. This leads to reduced shoulder and chest engagement, and increased strain on the wrists.
[[person performing a push-up with narrow grip]] [[close-up of hands during push-up]]

## The fix
Keeping the grip slightly wider than shoulder-width apart allows for a more natural, straight line from head to heels. This enables the muscles to work together effectively, engaging the chest, shoulders, and triceps.
[[person performing a push-up with wide grip]] [[close-up of hands during push-up]]

## Why it matters
Maintaining a comfortable grip width promotes proper form and reduces the risk of injury, allowing for a more effective workout.
