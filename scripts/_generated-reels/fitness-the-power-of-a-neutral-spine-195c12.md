## The Problem
Poor posture during deadlifts can lead to strain on the spine, hips, and lower back. It's easy to get comfortable with a rounded or arched back, even if it's not the most efficient position.
[[person deadlifting with rounded back]] [[gym training]]

## The Fix
Maintaining a neutral spine, with a slight outward curve, engages the core muscles and stabilizes the spine. This position allows for optimal weight distribution and reduces the risk of injury.
[[person deadlifting with neutral spine]] [[close up of back during deadlift]]

## The Benefit
A neutral spine during deadlifts enables you to lift with maximum efficiency and strength.
