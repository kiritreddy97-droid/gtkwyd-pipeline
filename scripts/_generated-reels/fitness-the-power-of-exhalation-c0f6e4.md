## The Problem
Lifting heavy weights can cause a phenomenon called "inspiratory hold," where the diaphragm and intercostal muscles are used to support the weight, rather than the targeted muscles. This can lead to inefficient lifting and increased risk of injury.
[[person lifting with poor form, holding breath]] [[weightlifting equipment]]

## The Solution
Exhaling during the heaviest part of the lift, when the weight is being lifted, allows the diaphragm to drop and the intercostal muscles to relax, freeing up energy for the targeted muscles. This is known as "expiratory loading" and can significantly improve lifting performance and reduce strain.
[[person lifting with proper form, exhaling during lift]] [[close up of person's diaphragm during lift]]

## The Benefit
By exhaling during the effort part of the lift, you're able to use your core and targeted muscles more efficiently, leading to a stronger, more controlled lift.
