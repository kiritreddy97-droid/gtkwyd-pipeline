## The Pressure
When you're in a plank position, your entire body weight is concentrated on your hands and arms. This can put pressure on your lower back, especially if you're not maintaining proper alignment.
[[person in plank position with poor posture]] [[gym training]]

## The Solution
Engage your core, squeeze your shoulder blades together, and pull your chest towards your hips. This creates a straight line from head to heels, distributing the weight more evenly.
[[person in plank position with good posture]] [[close up of torso during plank]]

## The Result
Proper posture during a plank can reduce the strain on your lower back, making it a safer exercise for your core muscles.
