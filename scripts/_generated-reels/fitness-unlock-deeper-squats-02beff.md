## The Limitation
Traditionally, the focus has been on warming up the major muscle groups, like legs and back. However, neglecting the ankles can significantly limit your squat depth.
[[person stretching ankle with a towel]] [[gym training]]

## The Fix
Warming up the ankles through calf raises, ankle circles, or calf stretches can increase range of motion. This subtle focus can help you achieve a deeper, more natural squat.
[[person performing ankle circles]] [[close up of ankle movement]]

## The Result
When your ankles are warm and flexible, your knees are able to track more naturally over the toes. This subtle adjustment can lead to a more efficient, deeper squat.
