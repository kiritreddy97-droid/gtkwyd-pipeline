## The Problem
Tightness in the lower back can stem from overactive muscles, poor posture, or even sitting for long periods. When these muscles are tight, it can lead to stiffness and pain in the lower back.
[[person sitting with slouched posture]] [[lower back pain illustration]]

## The Fix
The Pelvic Clock drill involves rotating the pelvis in a circular motion to loosen tight muscles and improve movement. This drill can be done anywhere, at any time, and is a great way to warm up before exercise or activity.
[[person doing the pelvic clock drill]] [[close-up of pelvis in motion]]

## Why It Matters
By loosening tight muscles and improving movement, the Pelvic Clock drill can help reduce lower back pain and stiffness. This simple drill is a great way to take care of your lower back and improve your overall mobility.
