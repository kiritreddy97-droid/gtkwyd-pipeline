## The mistake
Rushing out the door without a proper warm-up can lead to injuries and decreased performance. Many people skip the warm-up, thinking it's "just 5-10 minutes."
[[person running without warming up]] [[gym warm-up area]]

## The fix
Start with light cardio like jogging in place, jumping jacks, or cycling for 2-3 minutes. Include dynamic stretching, like leg swings, arm circles, and torso twists, for 1-2 minutes.
[[person jogging in place]] [[leg swings during dynamic stretching]]

## Why it matters
A proper warm-up increases blood flow, temperature, and muscle elasticity, allowing you to run more efficiently and safely.
