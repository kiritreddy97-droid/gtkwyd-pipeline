## Why it matters
Warming up increases blood flow, which carries oxygen and nutrients to your muscles. This surge in energy production raises your muscle temperature, making them more receptive to exercise.
[[increasing heart rate monitor]]

## The science behind it
As your muscles warm up, your capillaries dilate, allowing more blood to flow in and out. This increased blood flow brings oxygen and nutrients to the muscle cells, preparing them for contraction.

## The safe way to do it
A gradual warm-up, lasting 5-10 minutes, allows your muscles to adapt to the upcoming exercise. Avoiding sudden spikes in temperature and heart rate reduces the risk of injury or burnout. question everything
