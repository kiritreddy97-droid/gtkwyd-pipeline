## The Connection
Going to bed and waking up at the same time every day trains your body's internal clock. This helps regulate hormones, metabolism, and other bodily functions that affect performance.

## The Impact
Consistent sleep timing helps your body recover from exercise more efficiently. When you sleep at the same time, your body gets better at resting and repairing muscles.
[[person sleeping peacefully]] [[muscle recovery process]]

## The Result
Training at the same time every day can improve workout performance by up to 30%. This is because your body is in a state of optimal recovery and preparation.
