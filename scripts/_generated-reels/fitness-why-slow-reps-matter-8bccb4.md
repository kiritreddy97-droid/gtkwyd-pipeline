## The Problem with Speed
Fast reps can be tempting, but they often lead to poor form and ineffective exercise. Research shows that slower reps allow for greater time under tension for the muscles.
[[person doing fast reps with poor form]] [[gym training]]

## The Benefits of Slowing Down
Slower reps, on the other hand, give you more control over the movement, reducing the risk of injury. This also allows for a greater range of motion and more effective muscle activation.
[[person doing slow reps with good form]] [[close up of hands during slow rep]]

## The Science Behind It
When you move too quickly, you can't maintain proper form, and the muscles don't get the full benefit of the exercise. Slower reps give your body more time to engage the right muscles and make the most of the workout.
