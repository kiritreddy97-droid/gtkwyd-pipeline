## The Setup
A rich, velvety chocolate sits in a small bowl, its surface reflecting the warmth of the studio light. A cake, smooth and even, awaits the slow pour, its surface untouched and unblemished.

## The Pour
The chocolate is carefully lifted, its smooth edge gliding into the bowl, then slowly poured over the cake. The sound of the chocolate flowing, a gentle gurgle, is a soothing accompaniment to the visual.

## The Result
The chocolate flows, a smooth, velvety blanket, covering the cake in a satisfying, even layer. The final pour, the last drop, is the culmination of a calming, predictable process.
