## The setup
A pressure washer stands idle, its nozzle raised, waiting for the signal to begin. The pavement beneath is cracked and dirty, with streaks of grime and soap scum.
[[pressure washer nozzle raised]] [[pavement with grime and soap scum]]

## The clean
The nozzle lowers, and a stream of water shoots out, hitting the pavement with precision. The grime begins to lift, and the soap scum starts to bubble and break away.
[[pressure washer nozzle spraying water]] [[grime and soap scum lifting]]

## Why it works
The repetition of the water stream, the clean lines of the nozzle, and the satisfying sound of the water hitting the pavement all combine to create a sense of calm and order. The process is predictable, and the result is a clean and smooth surface, a true satisfaction to watch.
