## The setup
A freshly fallen log sits on a wooden stump, its surface rough and uncut. An axe rests against the stump, ready for one powerful strike.
[[log on stump]] [[axe resting on stump]]

## The strike
The axe comes down in one swift motion, splitting the log cleanly in two. The sound of the impact, crisp and clear, is part of what makes this moment so satisfying to watch.
[[axe striking log]] [[wood splintering]]

## Why it works
The precision and power of the axe strike create a sense of order and control, making the process feel strangely satisfying. The clean cut and sharp edges also contribute to the pleasing visuals.
