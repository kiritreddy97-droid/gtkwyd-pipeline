## The rhythm begins
A ball of dough sits on a lightly floured surface, awaiting gentle hands to shape it. The first fold brings the dough's edges together, a smooth, even motion that starts the dance.

## The rhythm builds
The hands begin to move in a steady, repetitive motion, folding and kneading the dough with precision. The sound of the dough being worked is almost hypnotic, a soothing beat that calms the mind.

## Why it works
The repetition of the motion, combined with the smooth, predictable sound, creates a sense of calm and satisfaction. The dough's transformation from a lump to a smooth, even surface is a testament to the power of rhythm and repetition.
