## The setup
A cake sits serenely on a flat surface, its surface lightly dusted with powdered sugar. A pastry bag stands ready, filled with a generous helping of creamy frosting.

## The application
The pastry bag glides smoothly across the cake in one continuous motion, covering it in a single, even layer. The sound of the frosting flowing and the soft scrape of the bag against the cake is a soothing accompaniment.

## Why it works
The repetition of the smooth, even motion, combined with the predictable sound, creates a sense of calm and satisfaction. The transformation from a dusty surface to a smooth, even layer is a pleasing one, and the process is both calming and meditative.
