## The setup
A stack of fluffy pancakes sits on a plate, perfectly stacked and inviting. A bowl filled with syrup and butter sits patiently below, ready to receive the pancakes.
[[stack of pancakes on plate]] [[bowl filled with syrup and butter]]

## The pour
The pancakes are lifted, and in one smooth motion, they are poured into the bowl. The sound of the pancakes hitting the syrup and butter is soft and satisfying.
[[pancakes being lifted]] [[pancakes pouring into bowl]]

## Why it works
The repetition of the pour, the smooth motion, and the predictable outcome all combine to create a calming and satisfying experience. The anticipation of what's to come is built up and then released in a satisfying way.
