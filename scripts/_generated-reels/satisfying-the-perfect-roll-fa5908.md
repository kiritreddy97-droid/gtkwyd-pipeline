## The setup
A freshly painted wall, still wet with the new coat, awaits its final touch. A paint roller, with the perfect amount of paint, rests at the edge, ready to glide.
[[paint roller close up]] [[paint-covered wall]]

## The roll
The roller glides across the wall in smooth, even strokes, leaving a perfect, seamless line. The sound of the paint being applied, a gentle whoosh, is a key part of the visual.
[[paint roller gliding across wall]] [[paint droplets falling]]

## Why it works
The repetition of the roller's motion, combined with the clean, predictable sound, create a sense of calm and satisfaction. The even strokes and lack of mess make this a visually pleasing and soothing process.
