## The ambiance
Soft candlelight casts a warm glow on a quiet room, creating a cozy atmosphere. Dim light signals the brain that it's time to relax and let go of the day's stress.

## The science
Dim light exposure helps regulate the body's natural circadian rhythms, making it easier to fall asleep. It's a subtle reminder that the day is over and it's time to slow down.
