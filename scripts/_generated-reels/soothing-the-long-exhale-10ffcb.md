## The Pattern
A slow exhale becomes a steady heartbeat, a gentle hum in the background. As the exhale grows longer, the body starts to let go of tension, one muscle at a time.

## The Science
A long exhale activates the parasympathetic nervous system, promoting relaxation. This natural response is like a gentle reset button, quietly rebooting the body.

## The Moment
As the exhale continues, the shoulders drop, the jaw releases, and the chest opens. In this quiet moment, the body finds its natural state of rest.
