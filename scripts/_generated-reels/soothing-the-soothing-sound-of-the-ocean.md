## The Sound
Ocean waves gently lap against the shore, a constant ebb and flow that never repeats itself. This unpredictability helps quiet the mind, as our brains don't know what's coming next.
[[ocean waves on shore]] [[seagull flying overhead]]

## The Science
The sound of waves is a type of white noise that can mask other distracting sounds. Research has shown that listening to ocean waves can lower blood pressure and heart rate.

## The Moment
As the sound of the waves washes over you, your breath slows and your muscles relax.
