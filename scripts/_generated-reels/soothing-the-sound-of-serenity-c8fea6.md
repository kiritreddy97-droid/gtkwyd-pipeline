## The source
A cat's purr is a unique vocalization that is both a sign of contentment and self-soothing. It is a sound that cats make by using their laryngeal muscles to vibrate their vocal cords.
[[cat on lap]] [[happy cat face]]

## The science
When a cat purrs, it is creating a low-pitched humming sound that is produced by the movement of air through the glottis. This sound has been shown to have a calming effect on both cats and humans.
[[cat in a carrier]] [[vibrating sound waves]]

## The effect
The sound of a cat's purr can be incredibly soothing to listen to, and can even be used as a tool for relaxation. It is a reminder that sometimes, the simplest things in life can bring us the most comfort.
