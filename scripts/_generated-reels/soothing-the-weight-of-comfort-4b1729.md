## The Psychology
Pressure can be a reassuring reminder that we are safe and cared for. It's a sensation that's hard to put into words, but it's a feeling we often associate with childhood.

## The Effect
As the pressure of the blanket increases, the mind begins to quiet, and the body relaxes further. It's a simple, yet profound, way to calm the nervous system.
