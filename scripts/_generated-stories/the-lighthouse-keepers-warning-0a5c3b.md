## The Watchtower
The storm raged on outside, wind howling through the small windows of the lighthouse like a chorus of banshees. Keeper Finley huddled in his chair, eyes fixed on the spinning light that illuminated the rocky shore. His thoughts were a thousand miles away, but the sound of the gale pulled him back to the task at hand. He checked the logbook, the entry from last night's patrol, and nodded to himself. All clear.

## The Watch
As the hours ticked by, Finley's attention never wavered. The light sliced through the darkness, its beam slicing the waves into a steady, white line. He had seen the occasional ship at sea, but tonight's vessel was different – a lone boat, its canvas awry, bearing down on the rocks. He knew the danger, had lived with it all these years, but something about this boat seemed different.
[[lighthouse beam slicing through darkness]] [[empty boat in distance]]

## The Warning
Finley leapt to his feet, grabbing the storm radio from the wall. "Mayday, Mayday, this is Lighthouse 17. We have a vessel in distress, heading straight for the rocks. Repeat, this is Lighthouse 17. We have a vessel in distress..." The words blurred out into the howling wind as he fought to keep his voice clear. He knew he had to act fast, but the radio crackled and spat, refusing to be heard over the storm.
[[lighthouse keeper on radio]] [[storm radio crackling]]

## The Call to Action
He grabbed his life jacket, rushed down the winding stairs, and burst out into the storm. The wind buffeted him like a ragdoll, but he stood firm, eyes fixed on the boat. It was getting closer, and he could see the sails torn, the oars splintered, the hull creaking as it rode the waves. Finley sprinted down the rocky shore, his boots pounding the wet stone.
[[lighthouse keeper running down shore]] [[empty boat in storm]]

## The Leap of Faith
The boat was a mere hundred yards away now. Finley could feel the spray on his face, the salt stinging his eyes. He launched himself into the air, landing hard on the boat's deck. The wind screamed in his ears as he scrambled to his feet, the oars splintering beneath his hands. He had to act fast, before the boat was crushed against the rocks.
[[lighthouse keeper landing on boat]] [[empty boat creaking]]

## The Rescue
Finley heaved with all his might, the boat shuddering beneath his back. Slowly, agonizingly slowly, it began to move away from the rocks. The waves crashed against the hull, but Finley's grip never wavered. He was a man and a lighthouse, a force of nature against the fury of the storm.
[[lighthouse keeper pushing boat]] [[rocks looming in distance]]

## The Calm
As the boat broke free of the rocks, Finley collapsed onto the deck, exhausted. The storm raged on, but he was safe, the boat safe. He looked up at the lighthouse, its beam slicing through the darkness, a beacon of hope in the heart of the tempest.
[[lighthouse keeper sitting on boat]] [[storm raging in background]]

## The Moment of Truth
The boat drifted closer to shore, Finley's eyes fixed on the rocks. He knew he had to get the vessel to safety, but as he looked around, he realized he was alone. No one was with him, no crew to help, no rescue team to call. It was just him, the storm, and the boat.
[[lighthouse keeper looking out to sea]] [[rocks looming in distance]]

## The New Watch
Finley took a deep breath, the salt spray mingling with the sea air. He reached for the radio, his voice steady as he called out for help. The storm raged on, but he stood firm, a sentinel of the sea, watching over the waves, and the waves watching back.
