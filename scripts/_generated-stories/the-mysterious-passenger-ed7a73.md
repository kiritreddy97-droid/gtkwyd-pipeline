## The ferry docks
It was a typical Wednesday morning on the ferry that sailed between the mainland and a small island. Jack, a grizzled ferry operator, was busy counting the passengers. He had done this routine so many times he could do it blindfolded. But something nagged at him as he called out the numbers. "Seventy-seven passengers, sir. We're good to go." As he handed the manifest to the captain, he couldn't shake the feeling that something was off.
[[ferry operator counting passengers]] [[ferry manifest on clipboard]]

## The discrepancy
As the ferry set sail, Jack's eyes wandered back to the manifest. It said 77 passengers. But he was certain he had counted correctly. He had counted the passengers as they boarded, one by one, and he was certain he had said 77. And yet, the manifest said 77, and he knew he had missed one.
[[ferry in the water]] [[ferry operator looking at manifest]]

## The search
Jack tried to focus on the navigation, but his mind kept drifting back to the missing passenger. He checked the roll call, but no one was listed as having exited. He called out to the captain, but the captain was too busy with the engines to pay attention. Jack was starting to get anxious. Who was the missing passenger?
[[ferry captain working engines]] [[ferry operator looking worried]]

## A hunch
As the ferry approached the midpoint of its journey, Jack had an idea. He asked the captain if he could take the helm for a bit, and the captain agreed. Jack started scanning the ferry, looking for any sign of the missing passenger. That's when he saw it - a small stain on the floor of the rear deck. It looked like a spill, but Jack knew better.
[[ferry operator scanning deck]] [[stain on floor]]

## The investigation
Jack called for the crew to gather around. "We need to figure out who this belongs to," he said. They all started searching the ferry, but no one knew anything about a missing passenger. Jack was starting to get desperate. He knew he had to find that person.
[[crew gathered around stain]] [[ferry operator looking determined]]

## A breakthrough
As they searched, Jack noticed something strange. The stain seemed to be leading to a small door hidden behind a panel. The door was locked, but Jack managed to pick the lock. Inside, he found a small cabin with a single bunk bed. And on the bed, a small note that read - "I'm not here."
[[ferry operator unlocking door]] [[hidden cabin]]

## The truth
Jack's eyes widened as he read the note. He had found the missing passenger. But who was it? He looked around the cabin, but there was no sign of the person. Just a small note that said - "I'm not here." Jack was confused. Was this some kind of prank? Or was someone trying to tell him something?
[[ferry operator reading note]] [[cabin empty]]

## The resolution
As the ferry approached the island, Jack couldn't shake the feeling that something was off. He had found the missing passenger, but he still didn't know who it was. And as the ferry docked, Jack realized that he would never know. The mystery of the missing passenger would remain unsolved. But Jack didn't care. He had found the truth, and that was all that mattered.
[[ferry docked]] [[ferry operator looking satisfied]]

## The aftermath
The ferry continued on its route, but Jack's mind was elsewhere. He had found the missing passenger, but he still didn't know who it was. He went about his routine, but he was no longer the same man. He had caught a glimpse of something much bigger than himself, and it had changed him.
