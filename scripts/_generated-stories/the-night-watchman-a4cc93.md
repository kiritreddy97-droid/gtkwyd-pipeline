## The Night Watchman
The museum's security team was the first to arrive at the Great Hall, and for Alexander, the night watchman, it was just another shift. The museum was closed to the public, the galleries dark and silent, except for the hum of the air conditioning. He checked the cameras and the doors, one last time, before settling in at the main desk.

## The Footsteps
As he walked the galleries, his footsteps echoing off the marble, he noticed a fainter sound, one he was sure hadn't been there before. It was light, almost like someone was testing the floorboards, but he knew the museum was empty. He concentrated, trying to pinpoint the sound, but it seemed to come from everywhere and nowhere at the same time.
[[security watchman listening for sound]] [[empty gallery at night]]

## The Closed Wing
He had been warned about the wing on the second floor, the one being renovated for the summer exhibit. The contractors had been working late, and the museum administration had deemed it off-limits until further notice. Alexander had always avoided that part of the museum, preferring the familiarity of the galleries he knew. But the footsteps were calling to him.
[[security watchman standing in front of closed wing door]] [[closed wing door with padlock]]

## A Flash of Light
He hesitated, then pushed open the door, calling out, "Hello? Is anybody there?" The only response was an echo, but as he stepped inside, he caught a glimpse of something out of the corner of his eye. It was a flash of light, blue and bright, like a camera's flash. He spun around, but there was no one there.
[[security watchman entering closed wing]] [[flash of light]]

## The Source
He moved cautiously, shining his flashlight into every corner, trying to pinpoint the source of the light. That's when he saw it: a small, hidden room, tucked away behind a tattered tapestry. He approached it slowly, his heart beating faster.
[[security watchman shining flashlight]] [[tapestry with hidden room behind]]

## The Explanation
Inside, he found a small table with a camera on it, the one he had seen that flash. The camera was aimed at the exhibit on the wall, a particularly sensitive piece of art. It was a prank, the kind of thing kids would do, but what caught Alexander's attention was the calendar on the wall, the dates leading up to next month's exhibit. This wasn't a prank; it was a preparation.
[[security watchman looking at camera]] [[calendar on wall]]

## The Curator's Secret
As he continued to explore the room, he found a journal belonging to the museum's curator, the one in charge of the upcoming exhibit. As he read through the pages, he realized that the curator was not just a connoisseur, but an artist, someone who had been using the museum as a studio. The renovation was not just about preparing the space; it was about creating a new kind of art.
[[security watchman reading journal]] [[curator's writings on page]]

## The Unexpected Gift
Alexander left the hidden room with a newfound respect for the art world and a sense of wonder. He realized that the museum was not just a collection of artifacts, but a place where creativity and innovation came to life. And as he made his way back to the main desk, he noticed that the camera was still set, the flash still waiting to capture the beauty of the museum.
