## The console
John hunched over the radio console, earbuds deep in his ears, as the night wore on. The static was almost hypnotic, but he had to stay focused. The night shift was long, and he had to stay awake. Suddenly, a weak signal appeared on the screen, and he zeroed in on it. The voice was distorted, but it was clear.
[[radio operator hunched over console]] [[static screen on computer]]

## The message
"Echo-12, this is... 'We are here. We are here.'" The voice spoke again, and again, each time repeating the same words. It was as if someone was stuck in a loop, repeating the same phrase over and over. John's curiosity was piqued. He tried to respond, but the signal was too weak.
[[radio operator listening intently]] [[static screen on computer]]

## The search
He scanned the frequencies, trying to find the source of the signal. It was as if it was coming from everywhere and nowhere at the same time. He tried to pinpoint the location, but it was like chasing a ghost. His mind was racing with possibilities. Was this a prank, or something more?
[[radio operator searching frequencies]] [[console screen with multiple frequencies]]

## The pause
Days passed, and the signal continued to repeat the same three words. John grew accustomed to it, but he couldn't shake the feeling that it was more than just a prank. He started to wonder if it was a message from someone who needed help. The repetition was driving him crazy, but he couldn't tear himself away from the broadcast.
[[radio operator sitting at console, staring at screen]] [[static screen on computer]]

## The discovery
One night, as he was listening to the signal, he heard something new. A faint voice, barely audible, spoke a single word: "Find." It was like a lifeline, a message from the other side. John's heart was racing as he tried to respond. He knew it was a long shot, but he had to try.
[[radio operator listening intently]] [[static screen on computer]]

## The breakthrough
The next night, the signal was different. The voice spoke again, and this time, it said something new: "Look in the attic." John's heart skipped a beat. Was this the break he had been waiting for? He quickly grabbed his notebook and jotted down the message. He couldn't wait to see what would happen next.
[[radio operator writing in notebook]] [[attic in dimly lit house]]

## The search
John rushed home, his mind racing with possibilities. He had to find the attic and see what was hidden inside. As he climbed the stairs, his heart was pounding in his chest. What would he find? Would it be the answer to the mystery, or just another dead end?
[[radio operator climbing stairs]] [[attic door creaking open]]

## The revelation
The attic was dusty and old, filled with trunks and boxes. John began to search, his eyes scanning the shelves. And then, he saw it: an old radio, exactly like the one he used at the station. It was as if the message had been hidden in plain sight all along.
[[radio operator searching attic]] [[old radio on shelf]]

## The silence
John sat in the attic, staring at the radio. The signal was gone, and the repetition was broken. He was left with only the silence, and the realization that sometimes, the truth is hidden in the most unexpected places. He smiled to himself, feeling a sense of satisfaction. He had solved the mystery, and found something more valuable than he ever could have imagined.
