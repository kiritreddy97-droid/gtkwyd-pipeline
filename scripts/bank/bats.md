## The only flying mammals
Gliding mammals like flying squirrels only coast. Bats are the sole mammals
capable of powered, sustained flight, with wings made of skin stretched over long
finger bones.

## Seeing with echoes
Many bats send out clicks and read the returning echoes to build a picture of
their surroundings, judging distance, size, and texture in complete darkness.

## Shouting without deafening themselves
Echolocation calls can be extremely loud. A bat briefly contracts a middle-ear
muscle with each call so it does not deafen itself, then relaxes it to hear the
echo.

## Most eat insects, not blood
The large majority of bat species eat insects, and many others eat fruit or
nectar. Only three species feed on blood, and they live in Latin America.
[[fruit bat eating]] [[bats in tree]]

## Major pollinators
Bats pollinate hundreds of plant species, including agave and many wild relatives
of bananas and mangoes. Some flowers only open at night for them.
[[bat feeding on flower]] [[night blooming flower]]

## Long lives for their size
A mouse-sized bat can live over 30 years, far longer than similar-sized rodents.
Researchers study them to understand ageing.
