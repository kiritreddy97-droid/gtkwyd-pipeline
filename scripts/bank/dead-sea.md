## Almost ten times saltier than the ocean
The Dead Sea is around 34 percent salt by weight, compared with about 3.5 percent
for typical seawater. That much dissolved salt makes the water far denser than
your body.
[[dead sea person floating]] [[dead sea shoreline]]

## Density does the work
You float higher in denser water. In the Dead Sea a person naturally bobs at the
surface and it is actually difficult to swim normally or push yourself under.
[[floating reading newspaper dead sea]] [[salt water surface]]

## No fish, hence the name
The salinity is far too high for fish or plants. Only certain microbes survive,
so the water is effectively lifeless.

## The lowest land on Earth
Its surface sits over 430 metres below sea level, the lowest exposed land
anywhere, and it keeps dropping.
[[dead sea valley aerial]] [[low elevation sign]]

## It is shrinking fast
Rivers that once fed it are heavily used upstream, and the shoreline has retreated
sharply, leaving sinkholes where fresh groundwater dissolves buried salt.

## Salt sculptures everywhere
As water evaporates, salt crystallises onto anything left in the shallows,
coating branches and stones in thick white crusts.
