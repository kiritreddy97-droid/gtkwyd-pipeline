## No brain, no heart
A jellyfish has a loose net of nerves instead of a brain, no heart, and no blood.
It senses light and gravity with simple organs around its bell and reacts
without any central control.

## Older than dinosaurs
Fossil evidence shows jellyfish-like animals swam the seas over 500 million years
ago, making them one of the oldest animal groups still alive.
[[jellyfish swarm]] [[deep sea jellyfish]]

## Mostly water
A jellyfish is roughly 95 percent water. Stranded on the beach, it almost
disappears as it dries.
[[jellyfish on beach]] [[translucent jellyfish]]

## A near-immortal species
One small species, Turritopolis dohrnii, can revert from an adult back to its
juvenile polyp stage when stressed, and start its life cycle again.
[[tiny jellyfish microscope]] [[jellyfish polyp]]

## Fastest sting in nature
Stinging cells fire a barbed thread in well under a millisecond, one of the
quickest movements in the animal kingdom, triggered by touch and chemistry
together.
[[jellyfish tentacles close up]] [[jellyfish drifting]]

## Blooms that shut down power plants
When conditions favour them, jellyfish numbers explode. Blooms have clogged the
seawater intakes of power stations and forced them to cut output.
