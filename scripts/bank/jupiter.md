## Bigger than everything else combined
Jupiter's mass is more than twice that of all the other planets in the solar
system added together.
[[jupiter planet space]] [[jupiter bands close up]]

## The shortest day
Despite its size, Jupiter spins once in under 10 hours, the fastest of any
planet. That speed makes it bulge noticeably at the equator.
[[jupiter rotating]] [[jupiter poles]]

## A storm older than telescopes could confirm
The Great Red Spot is a storm wider than Earth that has been observed for
centuries. It has been shrinking in recent decades.
[[great red spot jupiter]] [[jupiter atmosphere swirls]]

## Dozens of moons
Jupiter has more than 90 known moons. One of them, Ganymede, is larger than the
planet Mercury.
[[jupiter and moons]] [[ganymede moon surface]]

## An ocean world in orbit
The moon Europa has a global saltwater ocean beneath its icy crust, holding more
liquid water than all of Earth's oceans, and is a leading target in the search
for life.
[[europa icy surface]] [[subsurface ocean illustration]]

## A powerful shield and hazard
Jupiter's magnetic field is the strongest of any planet, trapping radiation
belts so intense they would be deadly to an unshielded astronaut.
