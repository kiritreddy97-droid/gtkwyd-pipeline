## A wobble in Earth's orbit
Earth's tilt and orbit shift slowly over thousands of years. Around 10,000 years
ago this pushed more summer sunlight onto the northern hemisphere, strengthening
the African monsoon.

## Grassland, lakes, and rivers
The stronger rains turned the Sahara into savannah dotted with lakes. Lake Chad
swelled to the size of a small sea, many times its current area.
[[green savannah africa]] [[large lake aerial]]

## Rock art of a lost world
Cave and cliff paintings deep in the desert show hippos, giraffes, crocodiles,
and herders with cattle, animals that could never survive there now.
[[sahara rock art]] [[ancient cave paintings]]

## It dried out fairly fast
As the orbital cycle reversed, the monsoon weakened. Over a few centuries the
vegetation died back and the desert returned, one of the clearest examples of a
climate tipping point.

## Fossil water underground
Rain from the green period still sits in vast aquifers beneath the sand. Some
desert farms and cities tap this ancient water today.
[[desert oasis]] [[irrigation in desert]]

## Sahara dust feeds the Amazon
Wind lifts millions of tonnes of Sahara dust across the Atlantic each year. The
minerals in it help fertilise the Amazon rainforest.
