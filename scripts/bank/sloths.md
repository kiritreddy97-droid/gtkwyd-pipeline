## Digestion in slow motion
A sloth's stomach has multiple chambers and works so slowly that one meal can
take weeks to a month to digest. Their diet of leaves holds very little energy.

## Built to hang
Sloths have extra neck vertebrae and a low muscle mass, with long curved claws
that let them hang almost effortlessly. Hanging upside down is their resting
state.
[[sloth upside down branch]] [[sloth claws close up]]

## A weekly bathroom trip
Most of the time sloths only come down to the ground to defecate, roughly once a
week. It is the most dangerous thing they do, because on the ground they are
nearly defenceless.
[[sloth on ground]] [[rainforest floor]]

## A tiny ecosystem
Sloth fur hosts algae, which gives it a greenish tint and helps with camouflage,
plus moths and other insects that live nowhere else.
[[sloth fur close up]] [[green tinted sloth]]

## Surprising swimmers
Sloths are about three times faster in water than on land. They can slow their
heart rate and hold their breath for several minutes while swimming between
trees in flooded forest.
[[sloth swimming river]] [[flooded rainforest]]

## Strong grip, even asleep
A sloth's hanging grip is so secure that sloths have been found still hanging
from branches after they have died.
