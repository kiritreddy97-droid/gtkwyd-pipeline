## The first night
Miss one night of sleep and reaction time, focus, and mood all drop noticeably.
The effect on driving is similar to being over the legal alcohol limit.
[[tired person yawning]] [[clock late at night]]

## Microsleeps
Push further and the brain forces the issue. It slips into microsleeps, lapses of
a few seconds where you are technically asleep with your eyes open and do not
notice.
[[eyelids drooping]] [[person nodding off desk]]

## The brain cleans house during sleep
While you sleep, fluid flushes through the brain and clears out waste products
that build up during the day. Skipping sleep lets that waste accumulate.
[[brain illustration]] [[flowing fluid abstract]]

## Memory suffers
Sleep is when the brain files the day's experiences into long-term memory.
Without it, new information is far harder to hold onto.
[[student studying tired]] [[notebook and coffee]]

## The record
The longest verified time a person has gone without sleep is about 11 days, done
under observation. They recovered, but had serious problems with focus, memory,
and mood along the way.

## Sleep is not optional
Every animal studied sleeps in some form. It is one of the few behaviours found
everywhere in the animal kingdom, which is a strong sign it is essential.
