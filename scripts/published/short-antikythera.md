## The find
In a Roman-era shipwreck off the Greek island of Antikythera, sponge divers
recovered a corroded lump of bronze gears. Nothing else this complex is known
from the ancient world.
[[ancient bronze gears]] [[underwater shipwreck]]

## What it did
Reconstructions show it modelled the motions of the Sun, Moon, and planets, and
predicted eclipses, all by hand-cranking a dial.
[[gear mechanism turning]] [[night sky planets]]

## A lost thread
Machinery of similar sophistication does not reappear in the record for well over
a thousand years. The knowledge was simply lost.
