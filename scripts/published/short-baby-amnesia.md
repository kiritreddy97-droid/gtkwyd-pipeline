## Childhood amnesia
Most people's earliest solid memory is from around age three or four. Everything
before that is a near-total blank, even though babies clearly learn constantly.

## A brain still under construction
The hippocampus, which files long-term memories, is still wiring itself in the
first years. Early events may be recorded but not in a form the older brain can
retrieve.
[[brain scan illustration]] [[toddler walking]]

## Language matters too
Before you had words for things, you had no way to tag experiences as stories.
Memory and language grow up together.
