## The finding
In classic experiments, people alone helped a person in trouble most of the time.
Add other bystanders and the response rate dropped sharply.

## Diffusion of responsibility
When many people are present, each assumes someone else will step in, or that
the situation must not be serious if no one else is reacting.
[[crowd on subway platform]] [[group of onlookers]]

## The counter
Studies show that if one person clearly names the problem and asks a specific
individual to act, the freeze usually breaks.
