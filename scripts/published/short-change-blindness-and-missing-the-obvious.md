## The illusion
We're wired to focus on the task, not the surroundings. Our brains filter out irrelevant information, making us blind to change.
[[person working on a task]] [[distracted environment]]

## The experiment
In 1994, psychologist Robert Shepard conducted an experiment where he asked participants to watch a person walk by, then suddenly change clothes. Half the participants noticed the change, while the other half didn't.
[[participant looking at the person]] [[no change detected]]

## The surprising truth
We're not just blind to change, we're also blind to the obvious. Our brains are designed to ignore what's familiar, not what's new.
