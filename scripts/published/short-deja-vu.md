## The feeling
Deja vu is the strong sense that a brand-new moment has happened before. It is
brief, common, and more frequent in younger people.
[[person looking puzzled]] [[busy street scene]]

## A memory glitch
One leading idea is that a new scene overlaps enough with an old memory to
trigger a familiarity signal, without the actual matching memory coming up.
[[brain illustration]] [[overlapping photos]]

## Your brain flags the error
Studies suggest the parts of the brain involved in conflict-checking light up, as
if the mind knows the familiarity is false and is double-checking itself.
