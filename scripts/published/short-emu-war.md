## The problem
After the First World War, thousands of emus moved into the wheat farms of
Western Australia, trampling crops and fences. Farmers asked for help.
[[emus running field]] [[australian wheat farm]]

## The deployment
The government sent soldiers with two machine guns. The emus scattered in small
fast groups and simply ran through the gunfire.
[[emu close up]] [[vintage military truck]]

## The result
After a few days and thousands of rounds, only a fraction of the birds were
taken. The operation was called off, and the press declared the emus the
winners.
