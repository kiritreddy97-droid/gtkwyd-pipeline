## The setup
The galaxy has hundreds of billions of stars, most with planets, and it is
billions of years old. Even slow ships could cross it in a few million years.
[[milky way galaxy]] [[exoplanet illustration]]

## The silence
So where is everybody? Decades of listening for signals have turned up nothing
clearly artificial. Physicist Enrico Fermi summed it up as a simple question.
[[radio telescope array]] [[empty night sky]]

## Possible answers
Maybe life is rarer than we think. Maybe civilisations do not last. Maybe they
are out there and quiet. Every option tells us something unsettling.
