## Two clocks disagree
GPS satellites carry atomic clocks. Because they move fast, their clocks tick
slightly slower. Because they sit higher in weaker gravity, their clocks tick
slightly faster.
[[gps satellite orbit]] [[atomic clock]]

## The gravity effect wins
The two effects do not cancel. The net result is that a satellite clock runs
about 38 millionths of a second per day fast compared with the ground.
[[earth from orbit]] [[phone map navigation]]

## Why it matters
GPS turns tiny timing differences into distance. Uncorrected, the errors would
pile up to about 10 kilometres of drift every day.
