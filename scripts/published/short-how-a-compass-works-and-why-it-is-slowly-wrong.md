## The magnetic magic
A compass needle aligns itself with the Earth's magnetic field, pointing towards the magnetic North Pole. The Earth's core is made of molten iron, generating a magnetic field that stretches out into space.
[[compass needle spinning]] [[Earth's magnetic field lines]]

## The wobble
The Earth's magnetic field is not fixed, it's constantly changing due to solar winds and geological activity. This shift causes the magnetic North Pole to slowly move, making the compass needle lose its way.
[[Earth's magnetic field map]] [[compass needle drifting]]

## The slow drift
As the magnetic North Pole moves, the compass needle will eventually point towards the South Pole, rendering it useless. It's a reminder that even the most reliable tools can be affected by the ever-changing world around us.
