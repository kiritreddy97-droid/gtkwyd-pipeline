## The Microwaves
Microwaves penetrate the water, causing the water molecules to rotate back and forth at the same frequency as the microwaves. This rotation creates friction, generating heat.
[[microwaves entering the water]] [[water molecules rotating]]

## The Plate
The plate, however, is a perfect insulator, with a low dielectric constant, which means it doesn't absorb or reflect microwaves well. As a result, the microwaves pass straight through the plate, without heating it.
[[plate as an insulator]] [[microwaves passing through plate]]

## The Result
The combination of the microwaves heating the water and the plate remaining unaffected creates the illusion that the microwaves are cooking the food, but not the plate.
