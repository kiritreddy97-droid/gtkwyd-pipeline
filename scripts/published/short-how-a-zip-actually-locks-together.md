## The Design
A zip consists of interlocking teeth that are held together by a spring-loaded mechanism. When you zip up, the teeth interlock, creating a secure bond.
[[zip teeth interlocking]] [[metal zipper teeth]]

## The Mechanism
The teeth are held in place by a spring that is compressed as the teeth are closed. When the teeth are interlocked, the spring is released, holding the teeth together.
[[spring-loaded zipper]] [[zipper teeth in place]]

## The Result
The result is a secure, airtight seal that is both strong and flexible. This clever design has made the zip an essential component of modern life.
