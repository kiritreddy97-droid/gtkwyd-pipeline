## The science
When a sound wave is played, it creates a node and an antinode. The node is where the air particles are stationary, while the antinode is where they are moving.
[[sound wave graph]] [[node and antinode]]

## The levitation
The standing wave creates a pressure difference between the node and the antinode. This pressure difference is strong enough to suspend small objects in mid-air.
