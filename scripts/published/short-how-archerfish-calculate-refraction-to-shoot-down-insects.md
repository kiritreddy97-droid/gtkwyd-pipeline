## The trick
Archerfish can shoot down insects with incredible accuracy, but how? They use a clever trick involving refraction to calculate the insect's distance.

## The physics
When an archerfish sees an insect, it calculates the angle of refraction using the difference in water surface and insect height. This angle is then used to adjust the water jet's trajectory.

## The deadly aim
By carefully adjusting the water jet's angle, the archerfish can hit the insect with deadly precision, often taking out multiple insects at once.
