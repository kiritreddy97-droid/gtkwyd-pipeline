## The Science
The bamboo's nodes, where new shoots emerge, act as shock absorbers. Each node is connected to the next, allowing the stem to flex and absorb the impact.

## The Result
The bamboo's segmented stem can withstand winds of over 100 mph. This incredible resilience has made bamboo a symbol of strength and flexibility.
