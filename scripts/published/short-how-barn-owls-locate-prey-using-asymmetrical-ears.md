## The asymmetry
You're about to see how a 2-3 cm difference in ear length can change everything. This unique arrangement allows them to pinpoint prey with incredible accuracy.
[[barn owl's head with ears]] [[sonar waves]]

## The mechanism
When a barn owl hears a potential meal, it uses its asymmetrical ears to create a 3D sound map. The shorter ear picks up sounds from the front, while the longer ear detects sounds from the sides and rear. This allows the owl to triangulate the location of its prey with remarkable precision.
[[barn owl in flight with sound waves]]

## The hunting advantage
This acoustic trickery gives barn owls a significant hunting advantage, allowing them to catch prey in complete darkness. It's a testament to the incredible adaptability of nature.
