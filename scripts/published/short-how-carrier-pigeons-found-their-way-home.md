## The incredible feat
Carrier pigeons can fly thousands of miles, crossing entire countries, and still manage to find their way back to their loft. Their homing ability is so impressive that it's been studied by scientists for decades.
[[pigeon in flight]] [[map with a small dot on a large map]]

## The secret to navigation
Pigeons use a combination of visual and olfactory cues to navigate, including recognizing landmarks, following the Earth's magnetic field, and detecting the scent of their loft. Their brains are wired to recognize patterns and make connections between different pieces of information.
[[pigeon perched on a landmark]] [[scent molecules in the air]]

## The remarkable memory
Pigeons have an impressive memory for their homing routes, which allows them to navigate even when they're released far from their loft. They can remember the location of their loft and the surrounding terrain with remarkable accuracy.
