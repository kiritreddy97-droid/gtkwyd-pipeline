## The pattern
Cicadas use a complex pattern of vibrations to communicate, but what if this pattern is also a defense mechanism? They seem to use prime numbers to create a unique code that's hard to crack.

## Why it matters
Prime numbers are a sequence of numbers that can only be divided by 1 and themselves. Cicadas use this property to create a unique "fingerprint" that's difficult for predators to replicate.

Researchers have discovered that cicadas use prime numbers to create a unique pattern of vibrations that's hard to predict. This clever strategy helps them avoid predators and stay safe.
