## The Speed of Change
You can learn more about the incredible cuttlefish in just 3 milliseconds, but can you see it happen? It can change its color in 3 milliseconds, making it one of the fastest in nature.

## The Science Behind
The cuttlefish has specialized cells called chromatophores that contain pigments. When stimulated, these cells expand or contract to change the color of the skin.

This rapid color change allows the cuttlefish to stay one step ahead of predators.
