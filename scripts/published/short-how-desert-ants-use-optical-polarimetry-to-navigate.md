## The trick
Can you imagine being able to navigate using the polarization pattern of light? By analyzing the polarization, they can determine the direction of the sun and the location of their colony.
[[ant walking on sand with polarized light]] [[polarized light pattern]]

## How it works
The ants use tiny crystals in their eyes to detect the polarization of light, which is then used to calculate the direction of the sun. This allows them to create a mental map of their surroundings and navigate back to their colony.
[[ant's eye with polarizing crystal]] [[sun in the background]]

## The ultimate navigation tool
By using optical polarimetry, desert ants can navigate even in complete darkness, without relying on visual cues. This remarkable ability has inspired scientists to develop new navigation technologies.
