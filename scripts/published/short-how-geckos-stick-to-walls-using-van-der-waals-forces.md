## The Van der Waals Force
Geckos have tiny hair-like structures on their feet, which increase the surface area for van der Waals forces to act. These forces are strong enough to support the gecko's weight, but weak enough to allow it to climb down.

## The Sticky Surface
The van der Waals forces between the gecko's feet and the wall are stronger than the forces holding the gecko's feet to its body. This is why geckos can climb up walls, but not down.
