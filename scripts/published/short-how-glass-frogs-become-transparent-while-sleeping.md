## The transformation
Can you guess what makes glass frogs' skin so transparent, and what that means for their survival? Their skin becomes translucent, allowing predators to see their internal organs.
[[glass frog sleeping]] [[translucent skin]]

## The science behind it
The frogs' skin is made up of tiny, overlapping scales that reflect light. As the frog relaxes, these scales spread apart, allowing the skin to become transparent.
[[frog's skin under microscope]] [[light passing through skin]]

## The hidden danger
This transparency isn't just a clever trick – it's also a potential weakness. Glass frogs can be vulnerable to predators when they're most vulnerable: asleep.
