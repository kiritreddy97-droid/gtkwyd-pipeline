## The phenomenon
How do lotus leaves repel water and dirt like a magic shield? The secret lies in their unique surface structure.
[[lotus leaf with water droplets]] [[water droplets rolling off]]

## The science behind
The tiny hairs on the surface of the lotus leaf create a hydrophobic (water-repelling) effect, causing water to bead up and roll off.
[[lotus leaf with magnified hairs]] [[water droplets rolling off]]

## The practical application
Researchers are now studying the lotus leaf's superhydrophobic properties to develop more efficient water-repellent materials.
