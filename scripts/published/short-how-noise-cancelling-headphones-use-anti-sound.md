## The anti-sound trick
Noise-cancelling headphones work by using a microphone to detect ambient sound, then producing a "negative" sound wave to cancel it out.
[[microphone picking up sound waves]] [[headphones producing negative sound waves]]

## How it works
The microphone picks up the sound waves, and the headphones then produce a sound wave that is the exact opposite frequency and amplitude.
[[sound waves being cancelled out]] [[calm environment]]

## The physics behind it
It's not just a simple matter of "making the sound go away", but rather using the principle of destructive interference to cancel out the sound waves.
