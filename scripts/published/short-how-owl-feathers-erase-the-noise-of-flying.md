## The special feathers
You're about to discover the surprising reason why owls can fly silently, using a simple yet effective design. These feathers have a unique structure that disrupts airflow, minimizing sound waves.
[[owl in flight with feathers spread]] [[sound waves disappearing]]

## The science behind
The leading edge of the feathers has tiny, serrated edges that create turbulence. This turbulence creates a region of low air pressure above the wing, reducing noise.
[[owl wing with serrated edges]] [[pressure difference]]

## The perfect silence
The combination of these features allows owls to fly silently, making them stealthy hunters. Their ability to fly quietly is a remarkable example of evolutionary adaptation.
