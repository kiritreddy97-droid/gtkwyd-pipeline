## The illusion
You're probably thinking perpetual motion is just a myth, but Einstein's work reveals a surprising truth. But the laws of thermodynamics say that's impossible.
[[perpetual motion machine animation]] [[clockwork gears]]

## The problem
Entropy, a measure of disorder and randomness, always increases over time. A machine can't sustain itself forever without increasing entropy.
[[entropy graph]] [[heat escaping]]

## The reality check
In 1905, Albert Einstein proved that perpetual motion is impossible.
