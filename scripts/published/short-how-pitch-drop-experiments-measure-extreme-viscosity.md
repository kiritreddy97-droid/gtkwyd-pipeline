## The setup
In 1927, a team of scientists in Australia dropped a small amount of pitch onto a glass surface, but it wouldn't fall. They repeated the experiment, and the result was the same.
[[pitch drop experiment setup]] [[pitch bottle]]

## The problem
The pitch was too viscous, meaning it had an extremely high resistance to flow. This made it difficult to measure its viscosity.
[[viscosity meter]] [[confused scientist]]

## The breakthrough
The team finally found a way to measure the pitch's viscosity by slowly heating it, allowing it to flow just enough to be measured.
