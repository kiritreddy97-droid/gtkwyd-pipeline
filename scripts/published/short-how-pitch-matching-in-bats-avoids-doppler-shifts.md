## The challenge
When a bat emits a sound, the returning echo is shifted due to the bat's movement. This Doppler effect can be misleading. The bat needs to accurately pinpoint the source of the echo to catch its prey.
[[bat emitting sound]] [[Doppler shift graph]]

## The solution
Bats use a unique biological mechanism to avoid the Doppler effect: they can hear the echo almost instantly. This is made possible by the bat's large ears and a specialized organ called the inner ear.
[[bat's inner ear structure]] [[fast sound wave]]

## The advantage
By processing the sound in a split second, the bat can accurately determine the distance and location of its prey. This incredible ability is the key to the bat's hunting success.
