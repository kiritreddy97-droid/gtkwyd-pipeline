## The trick
By changing pitch, a siren can trick your ears into estimating distance, but can you hear the fine details? The closer the siren, the faster it goes up in pitch, while the farther away, it slows down.
[[siren going up in pitch]] [[siren going down in pitch]]

## Why it matters
Our brains use this trick to estimate distance, making the siren sound closer or farther. The speed of the pitch change is like a speedometer for our ears.
[[speedometer gauge]] [[siren in the distance]]

## The illusion
This trick is so effective that it's been used in sound design for movies and video games. It's a clever way to create tension and make the siren sound like it's coming from all around us.
