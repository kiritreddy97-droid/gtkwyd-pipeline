## The illusion
You think you know how sea slugs move, but do you understand the physics behind their incredible underwater gliding abilities? But some species can glide through the water with remarkable agility. They use a unique form of propulsion, exploiting the properties of fluid dynamics.

## The science
As they move through the water, sea butterflies create a region of low pressure behind them, which pulls them forward. This is similar to the way an airplane generates lift, but underwater.
