## The frequency
How do sonar pings bounce off the 3,000 meter-deep abyssal plains? The answer lies in the way sound waves interact with water pressure.

## The physics
When sound waves hit the water's surface, they're refracted, or bent, by the pressure gradient. This bending causes the sound waves to bounce back, with frequencies adjusted by the pressure change.
[[sonar ping bouncing off water]] [[pressure gradient diagram]]

## The mystery
The exact frequency at which sonar pings bounce off the deepest ocean layers remains a mystery. It's a challenge for scientists to accurately measure and predict this phenomenon.
