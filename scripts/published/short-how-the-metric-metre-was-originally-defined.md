## The French Revolution
In 1791, the French National Assembly decided to create a new system of measurement. They wanted a system that was based on the decimal system and was easy to use.
[[French National Assembly]] [[decimal chart]]

## The Metre is Born
The metre was defined as one ten-millionth of the distance from the equator to the North Pole. This distance was measured using a meridian line that ran from the North Pole to the equator.
[[meridian line]] [[map of France]]

## The Standard is Set
In 1793, the metre was officially defined as the distance from the equator to the North Pole. This definition was used for over a century, until it was replaced by a new definition in 1960.
