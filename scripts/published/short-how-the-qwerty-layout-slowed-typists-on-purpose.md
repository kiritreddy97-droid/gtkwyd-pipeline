## The problem
In the 1870s, typists were typing at speeds of up to 100 words per minute. But with the introduction of the QWERTY layout, that number plummeted to around 20 words per minute.
[[typist typing on old keyboard]] [[speedometer with red needle]]

## The solution
The QWERTY layout was designed to slow down typing to prevent keys from jamming together on mechanical typewriters.
[[typist struggling with jammed keys]] [[typewriter with jammed keys]]

## The legacy
Today, the QWERTY layout remains the most widely used keyboard layout, despite the fact that it's no longer necessary for mechanical typewriters.
