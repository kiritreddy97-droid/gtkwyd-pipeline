## The Power of Warm Air
You're about to see how thermal soaring can lift an eagle all the way up to 10,000 feet. As they soar, the eagle's wings are no longer needed to generate lift.
[[eagle soaring on thermals]] [[rising column of air]]

## The Science of Lift
The eagle's wings are actually more suited for gliding than flapping, with a curved upper surface to produce lift. The shape of the wing, combined with the thermal updraft, creates an efficient way to stay aloft.
[[eagle in flight]] [[wing shape illustration]]

## The Eagle's Advantage
With thermal soaring, eagles can conserve energy and stay aloft for hours, making them one of the most efficient fliers in the world.
