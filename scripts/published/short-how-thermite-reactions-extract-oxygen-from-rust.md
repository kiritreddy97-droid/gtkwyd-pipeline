## The reaction
Thermite reactions involve a metal alloy and metal oxide, which release oxygen and heat. When metal oxides are heated, they decompose into metals and oxygen.
[[thermite mixture burning]] [[oxygen being released]]

## The role of thermite
Thermite reactions are used to extract oxygen from metal oxides, including rust. The reaction is so hot that it breaks the metal oxide into its constituent elements.
[[thermite explosion]] [[rust being extracted]]

## The end result
The oxygen released in the thermite reaction is what ultimately destroys the rust. The metal is left behind, clean and free of corrosion.
