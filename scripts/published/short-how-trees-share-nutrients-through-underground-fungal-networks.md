## The hidden world
How much of a tree's nutrient uptake comes from its own roots versus its fungal friends? About 90% of a tree's nutrients come from the fungi in its roots.
[[tree roots with fungal hyphae]] [[mycorrhizal network]]

## The fungal highway
The fungi form a vast network of tiny, branching tubes that allow them to share nutrients with each other and with the trees.
[[fungal hyphae connecting trees]] [[tree roots with fungal hyphae]]

## The tree's advantage
By tapping into this underground network, trees can access nutrients that would be unavailable to them otherwise.
