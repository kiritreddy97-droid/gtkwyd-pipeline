## The trigger
For you, the answer may surprise you: just 5-7 electrical pulses can trigger a snap shut in the Venus flytrap. Around 5-7 pulses, which is roughly 1-2 seconds.
[[electrical pulses on a graph]] [[venus flytrap trigger]]

## The mechanism
The flytrap's leaves contain specialized cells that can detect the changes in ion concentration caused by the electrical pulses.
[[ion concentration changes]] [[electrodes on a leaf]]

## The snap
The flytrap's trigger hairs and the electrical pulse detection system work together to create a lightning-fast response.
