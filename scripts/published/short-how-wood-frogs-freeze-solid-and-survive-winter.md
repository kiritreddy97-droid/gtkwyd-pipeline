## The freezing process
When wood frogs are cold, their bodies produce special proteins that make them solid. This is the only animal that can freeze its body tissues.
[[frog in a block of ice]] [[frozen frog in a block of ice]]

## The thawing process
In the spring, when temperatures rise, the proteins break down, and the frog thaws. It's a process that's still not fully understood.
[[frog thawing]] [[water dripping from the frog]]

## The secret to survival
The frogs' bodies can survive the freezing process because they have a low water content. This allows them to withstand the extreme cold.
