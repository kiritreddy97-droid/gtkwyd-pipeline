## The Pain of Loss
Losing $100 is like losing $200, but gaining $100 is only like gaining $50. Psychologists call it loss aversion.
[[person holding a $100 bill]] [[person holding a $50 bill]]

## Why it hurts
The reason is simple: our brains are wired to fear losses more than we value gains.
[[person looking worried]] [[person looking pleased]]

## The Enduring Effect
Loss aversion is why people are more likely to sell a stock they own than buy a new one.
