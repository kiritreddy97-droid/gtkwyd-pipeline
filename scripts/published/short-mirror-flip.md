## The classic puzzle
Raise your right hand and your reflection raises the hand on its left. Yet your
head still points up and your feet still point down. Why only one axis?
[[person at mirror]] [[hand raised reflection]]

## It flips front to back
A mirror actually reverses the direction toward and away from it. Your reflected
self is like a glove turned inside out along that one axis.
[[mirror reflection close up]] [[glove turning inside out]]

## Your brain does the rest
You imagine walking around to face your reflection, which would swap your left
and right. The mirror never did. Your mental rotation did.
