## The mystery
Roman sea walls and the Pantheon dome have lasted two millennia. Modern concrete
piers often need major repair within 50 years.
[[roman pantheon dome]] [[ancient roman harbor ruins]]

## The ingredient
The Romans mixed in volcanic ash and chunks of quicklime. Recent work suggests
those lime chunks are the key.
[[volcanic ash rock]] [[concrete texture close up]]

## Self-repair
When water seeps into a crack, it reacts with a leftover lime chunk and
crystallises, filling the gap. The material effectively patches itself.
