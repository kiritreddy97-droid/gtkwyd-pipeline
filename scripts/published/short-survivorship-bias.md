## The returning planes
In World War Two, analysts mapped the bullet holes on bombers that came back.
The holes clustered on the wings and tail, so the obvious fix was to armour
those spots.
[[ww2 bomber plane]] [[bullet hole pattern diagram]]

## The missing data
Mathematician Abraham Wald pointed out the flaw. They were only seeing the planes
that survived. The ones hit in the engine and cockpit never made it home.
[[fighter plane sky]] [[chart with gaps]]

## The lesson
Armour the places with no holes. Whenever you only study the winners, you can
draw exactly the wrong conclusion.
