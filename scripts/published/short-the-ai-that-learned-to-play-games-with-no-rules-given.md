## The Challenge
AlphaGo, a computer program, was trained to play Go without any prior knowledge of the game's rules. It was given a dataset of millions of games, but no explicit instructions on how to play.

## The Breakthrough
AlphaGo's creators found that the AI learned to play by identifying patterns in the game's data. It developed a sophisticated understanding of the game's underlying structure, allowing it to make moves that would stump human opponents.

## The Implications
This achievement raises fundamental questions about the nature of intelligence and the limits of machine learning. By demonstrating that an AI can learn to play a complex game without explicit rules, researchers have opened up new avenues for exploring the possibilities of artificial intelligence.
