## The problem
You've spent hours crunching numbers, only to have a single data point blow the entire analysis. The apex fallacy: a single extreme value can dominate the mean.
[[chart with a single outlier]] [[data points on a graph]]

## The illusion
Our brains are wired to focus on the outliers, not the average. The mean is often seen as less important than the median.
[[person looking at a graph, confused]] [[chart with a median line]]

## The correction
Recognize the outlier, and recalculate with a more robust measure. The median is often a better representation of the data. question everything
[[person recalculating on a computer]] [[chart with a median line and a smaller mean]]
