## The problem
What's the chance that a person who's been in a car accident is also a drunk driver? It's surprisingly high, but not because the two events are linked.
[[car accident scene]] [[alcohol bottle]]

## The mistake
Most people assume the two events are independent, but they're not. The base rate of drunk drivers is much higher than the base rate of car accidents.
[[insurance statistics]] [[probability graph]]

## The correction
So, if you're in a car accident, the likelihood of the other person being a drunk driver is much higher than you think.
