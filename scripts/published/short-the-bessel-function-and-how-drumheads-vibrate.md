## The pattern
Can you hear why drumheads vibrate exactly at 440 Hz, a frequency defined by the Bessel function? It's all about the Bessel function, which describes circular motion.

## The math behind it
The Bessel function of the first kind, J0(x), is used to model circular vibrations, like those in a drumhead. It's a fundamental equation in acoustics.

## The perfect pitch
The Bessel function ensures that drumheads vibrate at precise frequencies, producing the perfect pitch we hear in music.
