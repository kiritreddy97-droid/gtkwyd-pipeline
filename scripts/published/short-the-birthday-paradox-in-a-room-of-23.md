## The problem
In a room of 23 people, you'd expect to find at least two people with the same birthday. But surprisingly, the math says there's a better chance of finding two people with the same birthday than you'd think.

## The math
With 23 people, the probability of two people sharing the same birthday is actually quite high. It's around 50.7%, which is higher than you might expect.

## The surprising truth
In a room of just 23 people, the probability of finding two people with the same birthday is actually quite high.
