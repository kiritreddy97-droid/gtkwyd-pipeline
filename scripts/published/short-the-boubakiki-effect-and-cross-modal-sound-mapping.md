## The effect
In 1973, a German psychologist discovered that people associate certain sounds with certain shapes. Bouba, a soft, rounded sound, is paired with a rounded shape, while kiki, a sharp, angular sound, is paired with a sharp shape.

## The why
This effect is thought to be linked to the way our brains process language and sound. It's as if our brains are creating a mental map of sound-shape associations.
