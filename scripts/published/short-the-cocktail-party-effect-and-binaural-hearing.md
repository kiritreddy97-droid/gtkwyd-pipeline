## The trick
Your brain is surprisingly good at filtering out distractions, but what happens when you're trying to focus on just one thing? Research shows that our brains use binaural hearing to do this.
[[person in a crowded room]] [[two people talking]]

## How it works
When sound reaches both ears, the brain combines the signals to create a single, clear sound. This is called binaural processing.
[[ear with headphones]] [[neuron firing]]

## The catch
But what happens when you're trying to focus on a single sound? Your brain can still pick up the other sounds, even if you're not consciously listening to them. question everything
