## The problem
You're an expert in a field, but when you try to teach it to someone else, you struggle to explain it simply. You know the intricacies, but can't convey them to a beginner.
[[expert trying to explain complex concept]] [[confused student]]

## The reason
Your brain is wired to think in terms of your own knowledge, not the learner's. You're speaking a language that's familiar to you, but not to them.
[[expert speaking in jargon]] [[lost in translation]]

## The solution
Take a step back, and try to see the world through the other person's eyes. Simplify your language, and focus on the basics.
[[person explaining complex concept in simple terms]] [[question everything text]]
