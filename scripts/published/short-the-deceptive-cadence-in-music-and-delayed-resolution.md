## The illusion
You're listening to a musical cadence, but do you know what's really happening? Musicians use it to create tension and release, but it's easy to get caught up in the feeling of resolution.
[[music sheet with a cadence marked]] [[music playing with a cadence]]

## The psychology
Our brains are wired to recognize patterns, and a cadence is a familiar pattern that sounds like completion. But when we're not expecting it, the cadence can sound incomplete, even if it's actually just a clever trick.
[[person listening to music with a cadence marked]] [[confused expression]]

## The art of deception
A good composer knows how to use the deceptive cadence to create a sense of drama and surprise. And when it works, it can be incredibly powerful – but also incredibly misleading.
