## The illusion
When presented with two options, you're likely choosing the one that's not presented, but why? This is known as the decoy effect.
[[two options on a table]] [[person choosing one option]]

## The psychology
The brain is wired to avoid the obvious choice, even if it's the best option. This is because the obvious choice is often associated with less effort.
[[person looking at a more difficult option]] [[person looking at the decoy]]

## The design trap
Choice architects use the decoy effect to nudge people towards a desired outcome. But beware, the decoy effect can also lead to suboptimal choices.
