## The limit
Will you ever reach mental fatigue if you maintain a social network of 150 or fewer connections? This number is thought to be the maximum capacity of the human brain for social interaction.

## Why it's a limit
Dunbar's number is based on the idea that the human brain can only process a certain amount of information at any given time. Exceeding this number can lead to mental fatigue and decreased ability to form new relationships.

## The implications
Understanding the Dunbar number can help us navigate our social lives and avoid feelings of overwhelm. It's a reminder that quality is more important than quantity when it comes to our social connections.
