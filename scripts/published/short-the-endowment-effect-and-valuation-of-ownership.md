## The bias
When you own something, you start to see its worth in terms of what you paid for it, not what others would pay. The endowment effect is the reason you think your old car is worth more than a stranger's.
[[person holding a car]] [[price tag]]

## The cost
Valuing something based on its past is a costly mistake. The more you own, the more you overpay for it, just because you own it.
[[person holding a wallet with cash]] [[price graph]]

## The price is wrong
The endowment effect distorts your perception of value, making it harder to sell or trade. You'd be better off letting the market decide the price, not your own biases.
