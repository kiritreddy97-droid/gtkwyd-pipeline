## The illusion
Can you really trust your own prediction of what others think when 80% fail? About 80% of people overestimate how much others share their opinions.
[[group of people nodding]] [[social media screen with likes]]

## The reason
This is because we tend to see the opinions we already have, not the ones we don't. This creates a skewed view of what others think, leading to poor predictions.
[[person looking in a mirror]] [[thought bubble with conflicting opinions]]

## The truth
True understanding of others requires looking beyond our own biases. So, the next time you predict what others think, remember: you might be wrong.
