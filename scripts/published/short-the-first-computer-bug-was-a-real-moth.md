## The incident
In 1947, a team of engineers was working on the Harvard Mark II computer. They found a moth stuck in one of the relays.
[[computer room with engineers]] [[moth stuck in relay]]

## The solution
The team taped the moth to the computer log and wrote "First actual case of bug being found" next to it. They then fixed the relay and continued working.
[[engineer holding tape with moth]] [[computer screen with error message]]

## A lasting legacy
The term "bug" has stuck ever since, and is now used in computer science to describe any error or glitch.
