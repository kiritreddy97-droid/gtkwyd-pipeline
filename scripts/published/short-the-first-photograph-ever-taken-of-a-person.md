## The moment
In a small studio in Paris, a woman sat for a photographer, and a new era in portraiture was born. The subject was Sarah Anne Bright, the wife of the famous composer Michael Faraday.
[[Sarah Anne Bright sitting in the studio]] [[camera in hand]]

## The photographer
Louis Daguerre, a French artist and inventor, captured the image using his innovative daguerreotype process. This process involved exposing a silver-coated copper plate to light.
[[Louis Daguerre in his studio]] [[daguerreotype plate]]

## A game-changer
The photograph was a groundbreaking achievement, marking the beginning of a new medium for artistic expression. It paved the way for countless portraits, changing the way we capture and preserve memories.
