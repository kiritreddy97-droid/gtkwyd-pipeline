## The problem
You're still choosing 90% fat-free ice cream, but do you know why? Because the first sounds healthier, even if it's still mostly fat.
[[ice cream with "90% fat-free" label]] [[ice cream with "10% fat" label]]

## The psychology
Our brains are wired to respond to the right number, not the right amount. The 90% figure is more appealing because it's a positive number.
[[person looking at two numbers on a screen]] [[person smiling at the 90% number]]

## The takeaway
The framing effect shows us how our perceptions can be manipulated. Question everything, including the way we think about ourselves.
