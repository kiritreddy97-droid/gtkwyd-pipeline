## The problem
You'll lose memory if you don't recall what you had for breakfast and how often. The Google effect is a real phenomenon.
[[searching on laptop]] [[confused expression]]

## How it happens
When you search for information online, your brain is less likely to store it. This is because the information is readily available, so you don't need to remember it.
[[person searching on phone]] [[brain with a "file" label]]

## The cost
As a result, our brains are losing the ability to recall information from memory. This is known as digital amnesia.
[[person trying to remember something, but failing]] [[question everything text]]
