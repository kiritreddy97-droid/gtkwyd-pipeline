## The power of first sight
People who are physically attractive are often perceived as more trustworthy, competent, and likable. Research shows that this effect can influence our judgments of their character.
[[attractive person smiling]] [[person in a suit shaking hands]]

## The halo effect in action
If you think someone is physically attractive, you're more likely to assume they're intelligent, friendly, and honest. This can lead to poor decisions, like hiring someone based solely on their looks.
[[person filling out a job application]] [[person in a bad outfit]]

## The danger of assumptions
The halo effect can be a major obstacle to fair decision-making. So, be careful not to let your first impression be your last.
