## The problem
You get a raise, and your happiness goes up - but only until you get another raise. Psychologists call this the hedonic treadmill.
[[person holding a pay stub]] [[running treadmill]]

## The reason
The more you have, the more you want. And the more you want, the more you need.
[[person shopping]] [[price tag]]

## The solution
The key to happiness is not getting more, but getting better at being content.
