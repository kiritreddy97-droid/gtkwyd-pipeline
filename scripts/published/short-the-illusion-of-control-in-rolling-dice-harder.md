## The gamble
Can you spot the flaw in the thinking of 80% of people who claim to predict roll outcomes? About 80%, according to a study on superstition and probability.

## The illusion
The more you believe in your ability to control the outcome, the less you notice the randomness of the roll.

## The reality check
