## The problem
We're wired to seek patterns, to find meaning in the noise. Our brains crave explanations, but often settle for oversimplifications.
[[complex system with many moving parts]] [[oversimplified diagram]]

## The illusion
The more complex the system, the more the explanation tends to oversimplify. This is because our brains can't handle the complexity, so we simplify.
[[person trying to explain a complex system]] [[simplification filter]]

## The truth
Explanations are always approximations, never the whole truth. The more we learn, the more we realize how little we know.
