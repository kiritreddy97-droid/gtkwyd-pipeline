## The pattern
Why do people who are repeatedly exposed to a false statement become more likely to accept it as true? Research shows that the more you're told something, the more you tend to believe it.
[[repeated statement on screen]] [[people nodding in agreement]]

## The mechanism
Repeated exposure creates a kind of mental anchoring, where the initial false statement becomes the new normal.
[[person's brain being scanned]] [[neural connections forming]]

## The consequence
This can lead to the spread of misinformation and the erosion of critical thinking.
