## The paradox
You'd think that more data would lead to better decisions, but it often has the opposite effect. The less-is-better effect is a phenomenon where too much information can actually decrease accuracy.
[[researcher looking at data]] [[chart with declining accuracy]]

## Why it happens
When we're faced with too many options or data points, our brains start to filter out the noise. This can lead to a phenomenon known as "overfitting," where we become too confident in our decisions.
[[person overwhelmed by options]] [[computer screen with too many choices]]

## The hidden cost
The less-is-better effect can be especially problematic in high-stakes situations, like medical diagnosis or financial decision-making.
