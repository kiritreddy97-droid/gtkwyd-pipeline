## The quest
The Roman naturalist Pliny the Elder wrote about a secret recipe for a purple dye, extracted from the secretions of a rare sea snail. He claimed it was worth its weight in gold.
[[pliny the elder writing]] [[snail shell]]

## The ingredients
Murex snails, a type of sea snail, were the key to creating the dye, mixed with salt and vinegar to create a vibrant purple hue. The process was time-consuming and expensive.
[[murex snail farm]] [[purple dye vial]]

## The legacy
The Roman purple dye remained the gold standard for centuries, until the discovery of synthetic dyes in the 19th century.
