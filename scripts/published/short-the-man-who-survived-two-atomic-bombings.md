## The Unbelievable Story
Haruko Obuchi was just 2 years old when the first bomb fell on Hiroshima. She survived the blast, but her family didn't.
[[child playing in the park before the bombing]] [[devastated Hiroshima street]]

## The Second Bombing
Two months later, on August 9, 1945, Obuchi's family was in Nagasaki. The second bomb killed her mother, but not her father.
[[family in Nagasaki before the second bombing]] [[father holding his son after the blast]]

## A Life of Secrets
Obuchi never spoke of the bombings, even to her own children. She lived a long life, but the trauma remained hidden.
