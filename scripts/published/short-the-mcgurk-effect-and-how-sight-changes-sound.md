## The illusion
When you watch someone speak, you might not hear what they're saying. The McGurk effect shows how our brains combine visual and auditory cues.

## How sight changes sound
The visual information from the speaker's mouth influences the sound you hear. This is because our brains use both visual and auditory cues to process speech.
[[speaker's mouth moving with different lip movements]] [[confused speech]]

## The result
The combination of visual and auditory cues creates a new sound. The McGurk effect highlights how our brains are wired to make sense of the world.
