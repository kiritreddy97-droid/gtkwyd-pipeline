## The pattern
You're more likely to like a song you've heard a thousand times than a new one. Even if the new song is objectively better.
[[familiar song playing]] [[new song playing]]

## Why it happens
Familiarity breeds comfort, and comfort breeds preference. The more you're exposed to something, the more your brain associates it with feelings of safety and security.
[[person snuggled up with a blanket]] [[person exploring a new place]]

## The flip side
True expertise often requires exposure to new ideas and experiences. The more you're familiar with something, the less you'll challenge your own assumptions.
