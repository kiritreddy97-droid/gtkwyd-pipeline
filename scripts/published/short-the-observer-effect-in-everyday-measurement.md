## The strange phenomenon
When you measure a quantum system, its state changes just by observing it. This effect is known as the observer effect.

## What happens in everyday life
Even in seemingly classical systems, like a coin toss or a thermometer reading, the act of measurement can influence the outcome.
[[coin spinning in the air]] [[thermometer needle moving]]

## The power of observation
The observer effect is not just limited to the quantum realm; it's a fundamental aspect of how we interact with the world around us.
