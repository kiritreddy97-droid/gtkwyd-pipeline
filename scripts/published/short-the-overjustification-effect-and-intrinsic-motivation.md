## The paradox
When you're paid for something, do you enjoy it more, or less? Research shows that external rewards can actually decrease intrinsic motivation.
[[person working with reward stickers]] [[decreasing graph]]

## The underlying reason
When you're paid for something, you start to see it as a chore, not a passion. Your brain is wired to respond to rewards, but also to avoid them.
[[person looking at paycheck]] [[dissatisfaction expression]]

## The flip side
Intrinsic motivation is what drives true happiness and fulfillment. When you're doing something because you love it, you're more resilient and creative.
