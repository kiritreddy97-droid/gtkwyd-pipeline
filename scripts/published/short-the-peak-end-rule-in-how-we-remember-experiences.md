## The Rule
When we recall an experience, we tend to remember the most intense moment (the peak) and the worst moment (the end). The peak-end rule suggests that these two moments dominate our memory of the entire experience.
[[flashlight shining]] [[dark room]]

## Why it happens
The peak and end are more memorable because they're more emotionally charged. Emotions amplify the memory of these moments, making them stick in our minds.
[[emotional face]] [[memory book]]

## The Implications
The peak-end rule can be both a blessing and a curse. It helps us recall the most enjoyable moments, but it can also distort our memory of the entire experience.
