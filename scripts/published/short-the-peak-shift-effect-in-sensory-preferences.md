## The phenomenon
You've found a great restaurant, but the minute you're about to leave, the food tastes terrible. Why? The peak shift effect is a psychological phenomenon where our perception of a sensory experience changes as we're about to leave the source of that experience.
[[restaurant interior]] [[bad food plate]]

## What's happening
As we're about to leave, our brain is focused on the exit, and our attention is drawn away from the sensory details of the experience. This can make even the most enjoyable experiences seem subpar.
[[person looking at watch]] [[distracted expression]]

## The takeaway
The peak shift effect is a reminder that our perception is always changing, and what we experience can be influenced by our surroundings and expectations.
