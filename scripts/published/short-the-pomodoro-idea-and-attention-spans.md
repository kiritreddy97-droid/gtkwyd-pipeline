## The problem
In the 1950s, Italian psychologist Piero Alessandri created the Pomodoro Technique to boost productivity. But as our work habits evolved, so did our attention spans.
[[Pomodoro timer ticking]] [[person staring at screen]]

## The culprit
The constant distractions and notifications have shortened our attention span to just 8 seconds. That's shorter than a goldfish's memory!
[[smartphone buzzing]] [[goldfish swimming]]

## The solution
By working in focused 25-minute increments, we can reclaim our attention and boost productivity. But will it be enough to keep up with the pace of modern life?
