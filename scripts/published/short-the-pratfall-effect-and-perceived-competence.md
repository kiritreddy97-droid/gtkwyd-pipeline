## The phenomenon
When you fail at something, people tend to underestimate what you can do at other tasks. This is the pratfall effect, named after the comedian who famously fell on stage.
[[pratfall comedian]] [[audience laughing]]

## Why it happens
The brain is wired to associate failure with incompetence, but also with humility. When you fail, you show that you're willing to take risks and learn from mistakes.
[[person taking a risk]] [[learning from failure]]

## The paradox
The pratfall effect can actually make you seem more competent in the long run. When you fail, you show that you're willing to take risks and learn from mistakes.
