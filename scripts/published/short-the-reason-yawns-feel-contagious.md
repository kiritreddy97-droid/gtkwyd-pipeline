## The Brain's Social Cue
Yawns are a universal expression, found in humans and animals alike. They signal a need for rest and relaxation.
[[person yawning]] [[group of people yawning]]

## Mirror Neurons to the Rescue
Research suggests that mirror neurons, which fire both when we perform an action and when we observe it, play a key role in contagious yawning. These neurons help us empathize with others.
[[mirror neurons firing]] [[neuron diagram]]

## The Social Bond
Contagious yawning may be an evolutionary adaptation, helping us connect with others and build social bonds. It's a subtle yet powerful way to communicate and show empathy.
