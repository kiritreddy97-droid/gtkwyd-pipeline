## The illusion
When you see your rubber hand touched, you feel a touch on your own hand. Even if you don't see your hand being touched, you'll feel the sensation.
[[rubber hand being touched]] [[person feeling touch on own hand]]

## The brain's trick
The brain combines sensory inputs from your body with sensory inputs from the rubber hand. This creates a sense of ownership and control over the rubber hand.
[[neuroscience animation]] [[brain map]]

## The implications
The rubber hand illusion reveals how our brains create our sense of self. It challenges our understanding of body ownership and control.
