## The secret to success
Cats have a unique inner ear structure that detects even slight changes in gravity and orientation.
[[cat's inner ear]] [[cat falling]]

## How it works
The otolith organs in the inner ear detect the direction of gravity and use this information to make the necessary adjustments to the body.
[[cat's balance system]] [[cat landing upright]]

## The perfect landing
The righting reflex is made possible by the cat's flexible spine and muscles, which allow it to rotate its body in mid-air.
