## The effect
The first and last items in a list are more memorable than the middle ones. Research shows that the first item is 2.5 times more memorable than the average item.
[[list with highlighted first and last item]] [[person trying to recall a list]]

## Why it happens
The first item grabs our attention, making it more noticeable. The last item is often repeated or summarized, making it more memorable.
[[person summarizing a list]] [[person struggling to recall a list]]

## The flip side
The middle items in a list are often forgotten, unless they're particularly interesting.
