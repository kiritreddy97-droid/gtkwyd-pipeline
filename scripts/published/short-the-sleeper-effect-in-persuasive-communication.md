## The sleeper effect
When you're first exposed to a message, you're more resistant to its influence. But after a period of time, the message can re-emerge and have a stronger effect.
[[person watching TV]] [[subliminal message on screen]]

## Why it happens
The brain is wired to process information quickly, but also to seek patterns. After a while, the message can resurface and influence your behavior.
[[person looking at old photos]] [[pattern recognition]]

## The power of repetition
The sleeper effect can be harnessed to influence people's behavior. Advertisers and marketers use it to their advantage every day.
