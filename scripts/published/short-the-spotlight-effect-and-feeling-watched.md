## The illusion
You're the centre of attention, even when you're alone. This is the spotlight effect. Most people believe they're the focal point of everyone's gaze.
[[crowded room with people looking at you]] [[spotlight shining on you]]

## The reason
This illusion arises from the fact that we're all wired to be self-aware. Our brains are constantly evaluating our social performance, making us more sensitive to potential criticism.
[[person looking in the mirror]] [[critical voice in the background]]

## The uncomfortable truth
The spotlight effect is a fundamental aspect of human psychology. It's a reminder that our perception of ourselves is often at odds with reality.
