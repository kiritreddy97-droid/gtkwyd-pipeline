## The Stroop effect
When you're asked to read a word, like "red", your brain has to ignore the color "red" and focus on the word's meaning.
[[person reading a word on a screen]] [[red text on a blue background]]

## Reading interference
The color of the word can actually slow you down, even if you know the word's meaning.
[[person trying to read a word with a colored overlay]] [[slow motion effect]]

## The surprising truth
The Stroop effect shows us that our brains are wired to pay attention to the wrong things.
