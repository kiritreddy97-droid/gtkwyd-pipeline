## The phenomenon
You watch a crime scene on TV, and you're convinced the perpetrator is someone you've never met.
[[crime scene on TV]] [[stranger in the audience]]

## Why it happens
Your brain is wired to recognize patterns, and when you see a stranger on TV, your brain assumes they're more likely to be the culprit.
[[person watching TV with a thought bubble]] [[pattern recognition]]

## The impact
The third-person effect can lead to a distorted view of reality, making us more likely to blame innocent strangers.
