## The illusion of intelligence
Alan Turing's test has been hailed as the gold standard of AI, but does it really measure intelligence? It's a simple question: can a machine convince humans it's human?
[[computer screen displaying chat window]] [[person interacting with computer]]

## The limitations
The test only checks for one aspect of intelligence: language understanding. It doesn't account for creativity, common sense, or emotional intelligence.
[[Turing's original paper on a computer screen]] [[philosopher thinking]]

## The verdict
The Turing test is a useful tool, but it's limited in its scope. It's a snapshot of a machine's abilities, not a comprehensive measure of its intelligence.
