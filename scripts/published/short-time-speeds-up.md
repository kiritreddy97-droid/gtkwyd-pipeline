## Proportion theory
To a five-year-old, one year is a fifth of their whole life. To a fifty-year-old
it is a fiftieth. The same span feels smaller against a longer life.

## Fewer first times
Childhood is packed with new experiences, and novelty makes the brain lay down
dense memories. Routine adult days blur together and leave little trace.
[[new experience travel]] [[commuter routine]]

## The takeaway
People who keep seeking new places, skills, and challenges report that time feels
richer and slower in hindsight.
