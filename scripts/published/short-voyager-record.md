## A time capsule
In 1977, NASA attached a gold-plated record to each Voyager probe. It holds
greetings in 55 languages, sounds of Earth, music, and 115 encoded images.
[[voyager spacecraft space]] [[golden record disc]]

## Built to outlast us
The record is designed to survive for hundreds of millions of years in the near-
vacuum of space, far longer than any structure on Earth.
[[deep space stars]] [[earth from far away]]

## Still going
Both Voyagers have now passed beyond the Sun's bubble of influence into
interstellar space, and they are still faintly talking to Earth.
