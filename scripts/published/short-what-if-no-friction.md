title: What If Friction Just Vanished?

## Nothing would hold
Knots would untie, screws would spin out, buildings held together by friction
would sag. Your clothes would slide off and every pile of anything would flow
flat.
[[loose screws falling]] [[objects sliding]]

## You could not move
Walking works by your foot gripping the ground and pushing back. With no grip,
you would just slip in place, like standing on perfect ice.
[[person slipping ice]] [[shoes on smooth floor]]

## And could not stop
Anything already moving would keep going forever, because friction is what
normally slows things down.
