## The setup
A spinning top is a tiny, spinning top-like object with a heavy weight at the bottom. Its angular momentum is determined by its mass, radius, and angular velocity.
[[spinning top spinning]] [[torque meter]]

## The reason
The force of gravity acts on the top, but it's balanced by the torque from the spinning motion. As long as the top keeps spinning, the torque keeps the top upright.
[[spinning top stabilizing]] [[equilibrium sign]]

## The catch
If the top slows down, the torque decreases, and the top starts to fall. But if it's spinning fast enough, the torque is greater than the force of gravity.
