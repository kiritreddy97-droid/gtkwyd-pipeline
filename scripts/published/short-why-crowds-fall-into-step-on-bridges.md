## The power of the crowd
When people are in a crowd, they tend to follow the actions of those around them. This phenomenon is known as the "power of the crowd".
[[crowd walking in unison]] [[bridge with people walking]]

## The psychology behind it
The reason people follow the crowd is that it's easier to maintain our own pace when we're surrounded by others doing the same.
[[person looking around at others]] [[conformity in action]]

## The illusion of control
When we're in a crowd, we feel like we're part of something bigger than ourselves, which can make us feel more secure and less likely to question the status quo.
