## The ignition process
Without a spark plug, the fuel doesn't ignite until the piston reaches the bottom of its stroke. This is known as compression ignition, where the fuel ignites due to the heat generated by compression.

The diesel engine's unique design allows it to run without spark plugs, making it more efficient and powerful.
