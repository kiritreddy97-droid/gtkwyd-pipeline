## The Locking Mechanism
Flamingos can lock their legs in a tight ball, but how do they do it? The answer lies in the unique structure of their legs.
[[flamingo legs locked]] [[ankle joint illustration]]

## The Key to Stability
The locking mechanism is made possible by the shape of the ankle joint. It's a hinge joint that allows for flexibility, but also locks into place.
[[flamingo standing on one leg]] [[ankle joint diagram]]

## The Physics of Locking
When the flamingo locks its legs, the joint is in a state of compression. This compression creates a stable, rigid structure that holds the legs in place.
