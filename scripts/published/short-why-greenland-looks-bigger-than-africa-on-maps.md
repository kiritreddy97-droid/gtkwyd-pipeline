## The scale
The Greenland map is drawn to a scale of 1:10 million, while Africa is drawn to a scale of 1:50 million. This means Africa appears 5 times larger than Greenland on most maps.
[[map with Greenland and Africa]] [[map with Africa only]]

## The reason
The reason for this discrepancy lies in the way cartographers project the Earth's surface onto a flat map. Greenland's proximity to the North Pole means it is projected at a larger angle, making it appear larger.
[[greenland map projection]] [[africa map projection]]

## The illusion
This phenomenon highlights the challenges of accurately representing the Earth's surface on a 2D map.
