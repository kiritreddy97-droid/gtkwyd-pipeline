## The paradox
Why does hot water sometimes freeze faster than cold water? It's a phenomenon that has puzzled scientists and home brewers alike.
[[hot water freezing]] [[cold water freezing]]

## The explanation
When you heat water, the molecules gain energy and start moving faster. As the water cools, these fast-moving molecules create more collisions, which can lead to a faster freezing process.
[[molecules moving rapidly]] [[ice crystals forming]]

## The surprising result
In some cases, the faster-moving molecules in hot water can actually outcompete the slower-moving molecules in cold water, leading to faster freezing.
