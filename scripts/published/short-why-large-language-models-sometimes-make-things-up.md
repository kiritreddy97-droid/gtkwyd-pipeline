## The training problem
Large language models are trained on massive amounts of text data, but this data is often incomplete, biased, or outdated. This can lead to the model filling in gaps with fabricated information.
[[large language model interface]] [[data dump]]

## The self-reinforcing loop
As the model generates more text, it reinforces its own fabrications, making it harder to distinguish fact from fiction. This creates a self-reinforcing loop where the model's output becomes increasingly unreliable.
[[language model output]] [[recursive loop]]

## The human factor
Despite these limitations, humans still rely on large language models to generate text, often without fact-checking. This can lead to the spread of misinformation and the perpetuation of fabrications.
