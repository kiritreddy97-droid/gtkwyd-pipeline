## The technique
In the early days of cinema, animators used a technique called "drawn-on-film" to create the illusion of movement. This involved drawing each frame of the animation by hand, frame by frame, to create the illusion of movement.
[[artist drawing on film]] [[animation reel]]

## The problem
The problem was that the film was shot in a sequence that was the opposite of the desired sequence. To fix this, animators would draw the frames in reverse order, creating the illusion of wheels spinning backward.
[[artist drawing in reverse]] [[animation test reel]]

## The result
The result was a charming, if slightly disorienting, effect that has become a hallmark of old films.
