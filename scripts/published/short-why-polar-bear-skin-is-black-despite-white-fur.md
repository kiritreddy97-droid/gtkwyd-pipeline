## The reason
Why do polar bears have black skin, while their fur appears white? It's all about the structure of their fur, and the way it interacts with light.

## The science
The black skin absorbs heat from the sun, while the white fur reflects it. This helps the polar bear stay warm in the freezing Arctic environment.
