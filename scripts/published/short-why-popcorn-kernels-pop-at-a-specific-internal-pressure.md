## The critical point
The magic number 100 pounds per square inch is what makes popcorn pop, but what happens next? This is the magic number that turns a hard, dry kernel into a fluffy piece of popcorn.

## The science behind
The pressure builds up as the kernel heats and the water inside turns to steam. When the pressure becomes too great, the kernel's hard outer shell can't contain it.

## The perfect pop
The resulting explosion of steam and pressure is what makes popcorn pop. It's a delicate balance of heat, moisture, and pressure that's hard to beat.
