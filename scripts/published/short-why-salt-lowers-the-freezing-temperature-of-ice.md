## The Science
Can you guess how salt changes the freezing point of water, a process that's crucial for de-icing roads and food preservation? This process disrupts the formation of ice crystals, making it harder for water to freeze.

## The Chemistry
The chloride ions in the salt lower the freezing point of water by introducing impurities. These impurities, called solutes, alter the water's freezing behavior.

## The Result
The combination of salt and water creates a brine solution that can be cooled to a lower temperature than pure water. This phenomenon is crucial for applications like de-icing roads and preserving food.
