## The reason
To wrap your head around how sea otters stay afloat in strong currents, consider this clever adaptation. This clever adaptation helps them stay safe while they snooze.
[[sea otter holding hands with another sea otter]] [[kelp forest]]

## The clever trick
Their paws are wrapped around each other, forming a secure grip. This way, they can rest without worrying about being swept away.
[[sea otter's paw wrapped around another sea otter's paw]] [[currents]]

## The surprising benefit
This unique sleep posture also helps sea otters conserve energy. By not having to constantly swim against the current, they can rest.
