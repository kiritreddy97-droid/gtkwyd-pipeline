## The physics
Your ears are deceiving you - the speed of sound in water is actually 1,500 meters per second. When sound travels through a medium, its speed and wavelength change with temperature.
[[cold water with a stone]] [[sound waves on a graph]]

## The reason
The speed of sound in water is about 1,500 meters per second at 4°C. This is faster than in warm water, allowing sound to travel further.
[[warm water with a stone]] [[speed of sound comparison]]

## The nighttime bonus
At night, the cold water is at its coolest, making sound travel even further. This phenomenon is useful for underwater communication and navigation.
