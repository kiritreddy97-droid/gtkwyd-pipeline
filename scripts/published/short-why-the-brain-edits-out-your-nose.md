## The reason
Our brains are wired to prioritize facial features over other body parts. This is because facial features are crucial for social interactions and recognition.
[[close-up of nose]] [[distracted face]]

## The trade-off
The brain's focus on facial features comes at the cost of neglecting other important sensory inputs. This is why we often don't notice our nose, even when it's right in front of us.
[[nose in focus]] [[overlooked nose]]

## The evolutionary explanation
This phenomenon might have evolved to help us focus on social cues. In our ancestral environment, a nose wasn't as crucial for survival as facial expressions.
