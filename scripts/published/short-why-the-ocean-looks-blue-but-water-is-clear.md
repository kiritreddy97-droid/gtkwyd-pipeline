## The scattering effect
When sunlight enters the ocean, it encounters tiny particles like phytoplankton and sediments. This scattering effect bends the shorter, blue wavelengths more than the longer, red wavelengths.
[[sunlight entering the ocean]] [[light scattering]]

## The blue dominance
As a result, our eyes perceive the ocean as blue, even though the water itself is clear. This phenomenon is known as Rayleigh scattering, named after the scientist who discovered it.
[[ocean with blue hue]] [[clear water]]

## The illusion remains
This blue coloration is why the ocean often appears blue, even in shallow waters. It's a reminder that our perception of reality can be influenced by the world around us.
