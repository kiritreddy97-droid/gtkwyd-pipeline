## The problem
The conductive layer in touchscreens relies on the human body's natural electrical conductivity to register touch inputs.
[[touchscreen with bare hand]] [[gloves on hand]]

## The culprit
Gloves, especially those with synthetic materials, can disrupt this conductivity, causing touchscreens to malfunction or not register touch at all.
[[gloves on touchscreen]] [[fingerprint sensor failure]]

## The exception
However, some high-tech gloves, like those made from conductive materials, can actually work with touchscreens, blurring the lines between tech and nature.
[[conductive gloves on touchscreen]] [[question everything text]]
