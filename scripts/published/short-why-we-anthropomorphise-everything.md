## The ancient roots
Anthropomorphism has its roots in ancient cultures, where humans believed gods and animals possessed human-like qualities. Anthropomorphism was used to explain natural phenomena and the workings of the human mind.
[[ancient artwork with human-like animals]] [[ancient scrolls]]

## The cognitive shortcut
Anthropomorphism is a cognitive shortcut that helps us quickly understand and make sense of the world. By attributing human-like qualities to non-human entities, we can simplify complex behaviors and make predictions.
[[person explaining a machine]] [[complex machine with human-like controls]]

## The blurred lines
As technology advances, the lines between human and non-human are becoming increasingly blurred. We're now attributing human-like qualities to AI, robots, and even the internet.
