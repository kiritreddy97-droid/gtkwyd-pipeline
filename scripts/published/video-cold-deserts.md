## Dryness is the definition
A desert is any region that receives very little precipitation, usually under 250
millimetres a year. Temperature does not enter into it.
[[sand dunes desert]] [[dry rocky landscape]]

## The two largest are polar
By that definition, Antarctica is the largest desert on Earth, and the Arctic is
second. Both are locked in ice yet get almost no snowfall inland.
[[antarctica ice plateau]] [[arctic frozen landscape]]

## The driest place of all
Parts of the McMurdo Dry Valleys in Antarctica may not have seen rain for
millions of years. The air is so dry that ice sublimates straight to vapour.
[[antarctic dry valley]] [[bare frozen ground]]

## Hot deserts get cold at night
Without moisture in the air to hold heat, the Sahara can swing from scorching
days to near freezing nights.
[[sahara at night stars]] [[desert campfire dusk]]

## Deserts are expanding in places
Poor land use and shifting rainfall are turning some semi-dry grasslands into
desert, a process called desertification.
[[cracked dry farmland]] [[dust over dry field]]

## Rain shadow deserts
Many deserts sit just behind a mountain range. Air rises, drops its rain on the
windward side, and arrives dry on the other, as with the deserts east of the
Andes and the Sierra Nevada.
