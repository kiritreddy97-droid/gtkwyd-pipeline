## You lose bones as you grow
A newborn has around 300 bones. Many are separate pieces that fuse together during
childhood, so an adult skeleton has 206.
[[human skeleton model]] [[x ray of hand]]

## Bone is living tissue
Bones are not dry scaffolding. They contain blood vessels, nerves, and cells that
constantly build and break down bone. Your skeleton rebuilds itself over roughly a
decade.
[[bone microscope structure]] [[medical illustration skeleton]]

## The smallest bone is in your ear
The stapes, deep in the middle ear, is about three millimetres long. It passes
sound vibrations from the eardrum toward the inner ear.
[[inner ear diagram]] [[ear anatomy model]]

## More than half your bones are in hands and feet
Each hand has 27 bones and each foot has 26. Together your hands and feet hold 106
of your 206 bones, which is what makes them so dexterous.
[[x ray of foot]] [[hand bones illustration]]

## Bone is very strong for its weight
Bone can withstand large compressive loads, and gram for gram it holds up well
against steel. Its slight flexibility helps it absorb shocks without shattering.
[[strength test laboratory]] [[bone cross section]]

## Your marrow is a factory
Red bone marrow produces billions of blood cells every day, including the red cells
that carry oxygen and the white cells that fight infection.
