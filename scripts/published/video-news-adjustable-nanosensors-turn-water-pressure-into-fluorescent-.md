## A new way to measure pressure
Researchers have developed a novel platform that can measure hydrostatic pressure by converting pressure-induced molecular changes into fluorescence signals. This is achieved using a nanovesicle-based system. The platform is designed to detect changes in the molecular structure of a material in response to pressure.

## Pressure changes lead to light signals
The platform works by detecting the changes in molecular structure that occur when pressure is applied. These changes are then converted into fluorescence signals, which can be detected and measured. The sensitivity of the system is influenced by the stiffness of the material being measured.

## Stiffness affects sensitivity
The stiffness of the material being measured has a significant impact on the sensitivity of the system. The researchers found that the stiffness of the material can affect the magnitude of the fluorescence signal produced in response to pressure. This means that the sensitivity of the system can be tailored to suit specific applications.
