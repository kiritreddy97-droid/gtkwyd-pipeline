## Are common accommodations falling short?
Researchers at Florida International University have found that some widely used approaches to helping students with ADHD succeed in the classroom may not be effective. The study looked at how common accommodations, such as extra time on assignments and frequent breaks, impact students with ADHD. The results suggest that these accommodations may not be enough to help children succeed.

## What are the most common accommodations?
The most common accommodations provided to students with ADHD include extra time on assignments, frequent breaks, and a quiet place to work. These accommodations are intended to help students with ADHD focus and complete tasks more easily. However, the study found that these accommodations may not be sufficient to support students with ADHD.

## Are there other factors at play?
The study did not find that the accommodations themselves were the problem, but rather that they may not be enough to address the underlying needs of students with ADHD. The researchers suggest that a more comprehensive approach may be needed to support students with ADHD.

## What's the takeaway?
The study's findings suggest that simply providing extra time and breaks may not be enough to help students with ADHD succeed in the classroom. Further research is needed to understand the most effective ways to support students with ADHD.

## No single solution works for all
The study highlights the need for a more individualized approach to supporting students with ADHD. What works for one student may not work for another, and a one-size-fits-all approach may not be effective.
