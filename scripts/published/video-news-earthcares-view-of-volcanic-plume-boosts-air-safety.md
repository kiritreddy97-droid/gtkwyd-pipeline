## What is a volcanic plume?
A volcanic plume is a column of hot ash, gas, and rock that rises from a volcano into the atmosphere. It can be several kilometers high and can affect air travel by reducing visibility and posing a hazard to aircraft.

## Anak Krakatau's activity
Anak Krakatau, or Child of Krakatoa, has been active sporadically since it emerged from the sea at the beginning of the last century. Its activity has been monitored by scientists, who track its eruptions and the resulting plumes.

## Air safety concerns
The volcanic plume from Anak Krakatau poses a threat to air safety, and scientists have been working to improve our understanding of these plumes. By analyzing the plume's behavior, researchers can provide more accurate forecasts and warnings to pilots.
