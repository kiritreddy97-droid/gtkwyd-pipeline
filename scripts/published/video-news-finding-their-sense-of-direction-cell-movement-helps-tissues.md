## How cells decide which way is up
Cells need to figure out which direction is up to build tissues that work properly. This is crucial for creating structures like the inner ear, which relies on precise orientation to function correctly.

## The role of cell movement
Cell movement plays a key role in helping cells establish polarity, or a clear sense of direction. As cells move, they can sense their surroundings and adjust their orientation accordingly.
[[Finding their sense direction Cell]]

## The importance of coordination
Cells must coordinate their orientation with cells around them to build tissues that function properly. If cells get this wrong, tissues may fail to form or form improperly.
[[Finding their sense direction Cell]]

## The consequences of getting it wrong
If cells fail to establish the correct polarity, tissues may not form, or they may form in an improper way. This can have significant consequences for the development and function of the tissue.

## The process is crucial for development
Establishing polarity is a critical process that occurs during development. It allows cells to build tissues that work properly and enables the creation of complex structures.
