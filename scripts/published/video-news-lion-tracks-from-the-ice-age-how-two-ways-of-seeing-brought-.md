## Dramatic coastline changes
The coastline near Africa's southern tip changed dramatically over 400,000 years, shifting as much as 100km (62 miles) south of its present location. This drastic change exposed the vast Palaeo-Agulhas Plain, a habitat for large mammals. At other times, the sea level was briefly 13 meters (43 feet) higher than today's mean level.

## Ancient sea levels
The coastline's location varied significantly over 400,000 years, with the sea level fluctuating by 13 meters (43 feet) from its current level. This change had a significant impact on the environment and the animals that inhabited it.

## Palaeo-Agulhas Plain
The Palaeo-Agulhas Plain was a habitat for large mammals, which were able to thrive in the area due to the dramatic changes in the coastline. The plain's existence was only possible due to the shifting sea levels.
