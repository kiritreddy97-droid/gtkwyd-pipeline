## A nuanced view of math skills
For a long time, it was thought that math skills were primarily about cognitive abilities, such as working memory. However, a new study from the Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU) suggests that motivation plays a much bigger role in improving math skills.

## Why motivation matters
The study found that motivation is a more important factor in improving math skills than previously thought. This means that students who are more motivated to learn math are more likely to improve their skills, even if they have average working memory.

## Plateauing students
On the other hand, students who plateau in their math skills may be struggling with motivation. The study suggests that a lack of motivation can lead to a decrease in math skills, even if the student has average working memory.
[[student looking unmotivated]] [[student struggling with math problems]]

## The role of working memory
While working memory is still an important factor in math skills, the study found that it is not as crucial as previously thought. This means that students who have average working memory can still improve their math skills with the right motivation.

## Implications for education
The study's findings have important implications for education. Teachers and educators can focus on helping students develop their motivation and interest in math, rather than just relying on cognitive strategies.

## A more nuanced understanding
The study provides a more nuanced understanding of math skills and their relationship to motivation and working memory. By recognizing the importance of motivation, educators can develop more effective strategies to help students improve their math skills.
