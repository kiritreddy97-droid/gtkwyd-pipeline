## From multiple directions
Plants can grow towards light sources, even if it's coming from multiple directions. This ability is crucial for photosynthesis, as it allows plants to maximize their exposure to sunlight. Researchers have discovered how plants achieve this, despite having neither a brain nor a nervous system.

## Local sensing across the stem
The process of integrating light from multiple directions emerges from local sensing across the stem. This means that the plant is not relying on centralized information processing, but rather on individual cells and tissues responding to their local environment.
