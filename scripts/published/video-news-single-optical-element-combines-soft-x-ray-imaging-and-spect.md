## One optical element
Scientists have created a single optical element that can perform two different tasks: soft X-ray imaging and spectroscopy. This element, called a reflection zone plate (RZP), is a type of optical component that can focus and analyze X-ray light. By using this element, researchers can create high-resolution images of materials and analyze their electronic properties.
[[reflection zone plate]] [[X-ray light]]

## Two tasks in one
The RZP can perform both soft X-ray imaging and resonant inelastic X-ray scattering (RIXS) mapping. RIXS is a technique that helps researchers understand how the electronic properties of materials change as they interact with X-ray light. By combining these two tasks, the RZP can provide detailed information about the structure and properties of materials.

## Simplifying future instruments
The development of the RZP could simplify future instruments that need to determine both the location of structures in a material and how their electronic properties vary. By combining these two tasks into a single optical element, researchers can create more efficient and effective instruments that can analyze materials in greater detail.
