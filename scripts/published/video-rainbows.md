## Your own private rainbow
A rainbow is made by sunlight bouncing inside raindrops back toward you. The
exact drops involved depend on where your eyes are, so the person next to you
sees a slightly different one.
[[rainbow over field]] [[rain and sunshine]]

## Always a circle
A rainbow is actually a full circle of light centred on the point opposite the
Sun. The ground usually hides the lower half. From a plane you can see the whole
ring.
[[full circle rainbow aerial]] [[rainbow from airplane]]

## The double rainbow is reversed
A fainter second bow sometimes appears above the first, made by light that
reflects twice inside each drop. Its colours run in the opposite order.
[[double rainbow sky]] [[vivid double rainbow]]

## The dark band between
The sky between a double rainbow looks noticeably darker. It is called
Alexander's band, a region where little light is sent back to your eye.
[[double rainbow dark band]] [[storm clouds rainbow]]

## Moonbows are real
When the Moon is bright enough, the same effect can produce a pale rainbow at
night. It usually looks white because the light is too dim for our colour
vision.
[[moonbow waterfall]] [[night sky moon]]

## No pot of gold, and no end
Because the rainbow moves with you, you can never reach its end or walk under
it. It has no fixed position in the landscape.
