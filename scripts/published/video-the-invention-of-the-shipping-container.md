## Intermodal shipping
In the 1960s, the development of intermodal shipping revolutionized the industry. Intermodal shipping allows containers to be easily transferred between ships, trains, and trucks, making it faster and more efficient to transport goods. This innovation was made possible by the development of standardized container sizes and the creation of dedicated transportation networks.
