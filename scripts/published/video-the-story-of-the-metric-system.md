## The International System of Units
In 1960, the International System of Units (SI) was established to standardize the metric system across the world. The SI defined a set of base units, including the meter, kilogram, second, and ampere, which served as the foundation for all other units. The SI also established a set of rules for the use of units, including the use of prefixes and suffixes to indicate multiples and submultiples.
