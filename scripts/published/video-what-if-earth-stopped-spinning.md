## We are all moving fast
At the equator the ground is carrying you eastward at about 1,600 kilometres per
hour. You do not feel it because everything around you moves at the same speed.
[[earth rotating space]] [[equator landscape]]

## A sudden stop is not realistic
Nothing could halt the planet instantly. But imagine it slowed to a stop over
many years. That version is a useful thought experiment.
[[globe spinning slowing]] [[earth from orbit]]

## The oceans would move
Earth's spin makes the planet bulge at the equator and pushes water there. Take
the spin away and the seas would gradually flow toward the poles, leaving a huge
band of dry land around the middle.
[[ocean waves aerial]] [[polar ice cap]]

## One day, one year long
Without rotation, a spot on Earth would face the Sun for about six months, then
face away for six months. Days and nights would each last half a year.
[[long sunset horizon]] [[dark frozen landscape]]

## The magnetic shield weakens
Earth's churning molten core, helped by rotation, generates the magnetic field
that deflects solar particles. A still planet would slowly lose that protection.
[[magnetic field illustration]] [[solar wind particles]]

## The atmosphere would settle
Winds driven by the spin would fade, weather patterns would reorganise
completely, and the planet would become a very different, and much harsher,
place to live.
