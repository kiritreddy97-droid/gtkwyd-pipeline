## They eat a special food
Flamingos get their distinctive pink colour from carotenoid pigments found in the algae and small crustaceans they eat. These pigments are derived from the food they consume, particularly brine shrimp and algae, which contain high levels of astaxanthin and canthaxanthin. When flamingos eat these foods, the pigments are absorbed into their bloodstream and deposited into their feathers, giving them their characteristic pink colour.

The intensity of a flamingo's pink colour depends on the amount of carotenoid-rich food it consumes.
