## A piece of the puzzle
Researchers analyzed the color pigments in a historical painting signed "L daVinci." Most of the pigments are consistent with materials used during the Renaissance.
[[painting on canvas]] [[art studio with artist]]

## Pigments from the past
The study found that most of the pigments match the materials used during the Renaissance. This suggests that the painting was created during this time period.
[[artist's palette]] [[pigment jars]]

## A hidden clue
However, a more recent pigment appears to be linked to later alterations. This discovery provides new insights into the painting's history.
[[painting with altered areas]] [[restoration workshop]]

## Materials science to the rescue
The study demonstrates how materials science can provide new pieces of the puzzle behind the history of a work of art. By analyzing the pigments, researchers can learn more about the painting's creation and any subsequent changes.
