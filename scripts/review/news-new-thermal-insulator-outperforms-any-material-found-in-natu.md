## What makes it special
The new material is an extreme thermal insulator, meaning it's very good at keeping heat in or out. It's also exceptionally stiff, which is unusual for a material that's also very good at insulating heat.

## How it's made
The material can be printed as a thin film at large scales, which is unusual for a material that's also very stiff.

## How it compares
The material has one of the lowest thermal conductivity profiles of any dense, or nonporous, material. This means it's very good at keeping heat in or out, approaching the theoretical limit of how good a material can be as a thermal insulator.
