## The trick
A tiny mic listens to outside noise thousands of times a second, then the
earbud plays an inverted copy of that exact sound wave.
[[earbuds close up]] [[sound wave animation]]

## Wave meets wave
The real noise and the flipped copy arrive at your eardrum together and
cancel each other out, leaving near silence behind.
[[person wearing earbuds]] [[cancelling waveform diagram]]

## It works best on drones
Steady sounds like engine hum cancel almost perfectly. Sudden sharp sounds are
too fast to predict and cancel in time.
