## The doubling
You have 2 parents, 4 grandparents, 8 great-grandparents. Keep doubling for about
40 generations and you reach over a trillion slots.
[[family tree chart animation]] [[vintage sepia family photograph]]

## The impossible number
Only around 100 billion humans have ever lived. A trillion ancestor slots cannot
be a trillion different people.
[[crowd of people]] [[historical population illustration]]

## Pedigree collapse
The answer is that the same ancestors fill many slots. Go back far enough and
almost everyone alive then who has descendants today is an ancestor of everyone
now.
