## Selective attention
In a noisy room you can lock onto one voice and treat the rest as background.
Your brain is filtering the sound, not your ears.
[[crowded party talking]] [[person listening intently]]

## The name trigger
Even while you focus elsewhere, part of your brain keeps scanning the ignored
streams. Highly relevant words, especially your own name, can break through.
[[people mingling]] [[sound wave visualization]]

## Why it is hard for machines
Separating overlapping voices is still a difficult problem for computers, which
is why voice assistants struggle in a crowd.
