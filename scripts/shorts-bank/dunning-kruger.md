## The pattern
Psychologists David Dunning and Justin Kruger found that people who scored
lowest on a test were the most likely to massively overrate how well they had
done.
[[confident person talking]] [[graph rising line]]

## Why it happens
The skill required to be good at something is the same skill required to spot
your own mistakes. Missing one means missing the other, so the errors stay
invisible.

## The flip side
Genuine experts often underrate themselves, because they assume things that feel
easy to them are easy for everyone.
