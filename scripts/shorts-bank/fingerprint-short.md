## Not a photo
The scanner records ridge patterns as a mathematical template, not an image
anyone could recognise.
[[fingerprint scan phone]] [[fingerprint pattern close up]]

## A fresh check every time
Each unlock builds a brand new template and compares it against the saved
one, looking for a close match.
[[phone unlocking]] [[security icon animation]]

## Locked in its own vault
That template lives in an isolated chip, separate from the rest of the
phone's software.
