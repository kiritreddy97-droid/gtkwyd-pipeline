## Three types
Astronomer Nikolai Kardashev proposed ranking civilisations by the energy they
command. A Type 1 uses all the energy of its planet, a Type 2 its whole star, a
Type 3 its entire galaxy.
[[planet earth from space]] [[sun close up]]

## Where we sit
By one common estimate, humanity is around 0.7 on the scale, still tapping a
fraction of the sunlight and heat reaching Earth.
[[solar power field]] [[city lights night]]

## The point
The scale is a thought experiment, not a prediction. It reframes progress as a
question of energy, not gadgets.
