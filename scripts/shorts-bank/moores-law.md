## The observation
Gordon Moore noticed the number of transistors on a chip was roughly doubling
every year, later revised to about every two years, for the same cost.
[[computer chip macro]] [[circuit board close up]]

## A self-fulfilling target
It was never a law of physics. It became a shared roadmap, and the whole
industry organised its research and budgets around hitting it.
[[semiconductor factory clean room]] [[silicon wafer]]

## The slowdown
Transistors are now only atoms wide, and the classic doubling has stalled.
Progress continues through new chip designs and stacking, not just shrinking.
