## The effect
Pareidolia is the tendency to see meaningful shapes, usually faces, in random
patterns. Power sockets, car fronts, and the Moon all get read as faces.
[[face in electrical socket]] [[clouds shapes sky]]

## A dedicated face detector
A patch of the brain called the fusiform face area responds to face-like
arrangements in a fraction of a second, before you consciously look.
[[brain scan illustration]] [[car front headlights]]

## Why it stuck around
Missing a real face is costly. Seeing one that is not there is harmless. Evolution
tuned the system to over-detect.
