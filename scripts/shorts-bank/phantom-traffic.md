## The ghost jam
On a busy motorway, one driver taps the brakes. The car behind brakes a little
harder, and the next harder still. A stopping wave travels backward down the
road.
[[highway traffic aerial]] [[cars braking]]

## A wave, not a place
That jam can crawl backward against the flow of cars for kilometres and last for
an hour, long after the original driver has left.
[[traffic wave illustration]] [[congested motorway]]

## The fix
Experiments show that if drivers leave bigger gaps and avoid sharp braking, the
waves never build. Smooth driving keeps everyone moving.
