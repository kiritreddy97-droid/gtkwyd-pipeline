## Not the oxygen
When you hold your breath, that burning need to inhale is not your body sensing
low oxygen. It is sensing rising carbon dioxide in your blood.
[[person holding breath]] [[lungs illustration]]

## The CO2 alarm
Sensors in your brain stem track carbon dioxide very closely. As it climbs, they
trigger a stronger and stronger drive to breathe that is almost impossible to
override.
[[brain stem diagram]] [[diver underwater]]

## The freediver trick
Freedivers breathe out fully and use techniques to blunt that CO2 signal, which
is also why doing it without training is dangerous.
