## Two coils
A coil in the pad and a coil in your phone face each other, close but never
touching.
[[phone on wireless charger]] [[coil close up]]

## An invisible bridge
Current in the pad creates a magnetic field that induces its own current in
the phone's coil, charging the battery.
[[magnetic field animation]] [[phone charging]]

## Why position matters
Line the coils up and it charges fast. Slide off centre and the connection
weakens fast too.
