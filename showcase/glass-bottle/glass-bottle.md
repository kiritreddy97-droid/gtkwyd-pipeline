## Sand
Every glass bottle starts as plain sand.
<<clip:scene1.mp4>>
{{3D render, golden sand pouring into a giant industrial furnace hopper, warm light}}

## Furnace
Sand, soda ash and limestone melt together at about fifteen hundred degrees.
<<clip:scene2.mp4>>
{{3D render, inside a glowing industrial glass furnace, bright orange molten glass}}

## Gob
A glowing blob of molten glass, called a gob, drops into a mold.
<<clip:scene3.mp4>>
{{3D render, a glowing orange gob of molten glass falling into a metal bottle mold}}

## Blow
Compressed air blows it into the shape of a bottle in seconds.
<<clip:scene4.mp4>>
{{3D render, a metal mold opening to reveal a glowing hot glass bottle, steam}}

## Cooling
Cooled, checked and packed, it is ready to hold your drink.
<<clip:scene5.mp4>>
{{3D render, rows of clear glass bottles moving on a factory conveyor line}}
